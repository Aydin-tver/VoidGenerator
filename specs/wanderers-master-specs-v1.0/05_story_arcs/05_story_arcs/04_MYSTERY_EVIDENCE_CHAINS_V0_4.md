# Wanderers — Mystery & Evidence Chains v0.4

## Core mystery
Why do apparently ordinary navigation/registry/technical anomalies correlate with systems associated with SIGMA, the Thread and the deeper historical conflict?

## Evidence layers
### E0 — Observable anomaly
A route/registry/technical result does not match expected behavior.

### E1 — Human/institutional explanation
Someone may have altered, corrected or withheld information.

### E2 — Cross-source contradiction
Independent evidence suggests the anomaly is not a single local mistake.

### E3 — Algorithmic connection
Evidence begins connecting anomalies to the historical algorithm conflict.

### E4 — Thread connection
Resonance provides a second class of evidence, without proving the final interpretation.

### E5 — Identity implication
The player discovers that conscious-system identity is part of the larger question.

### E6 — Voidari breadcrumb
A clue suggests that transfer/continuity research existed, without fully exposing the future technology.

## Evidence rules
- Evidence is not automatically truth.
- Evidence has source, confidence, interpretation status and prerequisites.
- Contradictory evidence is allowed.
- Major revelation requires multiple independent clues.
- Every major mystery fact needs at least one playable acquisition path.

## Mystery board structure
Question → known evidence → competing interpretations → missing evidence → next playable lead.

## No GPS rule
Mystery navigation uses information, station/region knowledge and mission context. It must not depend on artificial quest-point pointers.
