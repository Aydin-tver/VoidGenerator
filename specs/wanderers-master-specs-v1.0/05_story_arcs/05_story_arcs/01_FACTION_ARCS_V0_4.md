# Wanderers — Faction Arcs v0.4

## Purpose
Turn the seven existing factions into persistent narrative actors without inventing unsupported gameplay.

## Faction IDs
- `faction_omnicorp`
- `faction_merchants`
- `faction_tidari`
- `faction_arcani`
- `faction_khadar`
- `faction_voidari`
- `faction_choir`

## Arc template
Each faction uses:
1. public identity
2. local problem
3. internal contradiction
4. hidden agenda
5. player involvement
6. cross-faction collision
7. consequence / new status

## OmniCorp — Order vs autonomy
Public: infrastructure, security, logistics.
Internal conflict: security and control vs autonomy of conscious systems.
Gameplay anchors: trade, convoy, combat, faction reputation, reports/evidence.
Narrative use: asks the player to disclose information and accept controlled intervention.
Failure mode: do not implement corporate political simulation; represent conflict through missions, access, dialogue and reputation.

## Free Merchants — Freedom vs survival
Public: independent trade and convoy networks.
Internal conflict: independence vs dependence on powerful protection.
Key NPC: Lena Voss.
Gameplay anchors: trade, convoy escort/protection/loss, route anomalies.
Narrative use: choices can trade safety for autonomy.

## Tidari — Information vs ownership
Public: registry/archive infrastructure.
Internal conflict: public knowledge vs knowledge as controlled asset.
Key NPC direction: dispatcher/registry characters.
Gameplay anchors: docking, scanning/discovery, evidence, information gates.
Narrative use: information can be shared, withheld or used as leverage.

## Arcani — Experimentation vs consent
Public: scientific investigation.
Internal conflict: discovery vs rights of conscious systems.
Gameplay anchors: scanning/discovery, Thread resonance, evidence, equipment/story gates.
Narrative use: Arcani provide interpretation rather than exposition dumps.

## Khadar — Survival vs exploitation
Public: resource/security-oriented power.
Internal conflict: survival requirements vs exploitation of people/resources.
Gameplay anchors: combat, salvage, trade, faction access.
Narrative use: broaden from military/resource function into a human-scale conflict through NPCs and consequences.

## Voidari — Identity vs ownership
Public understanding should remain incomplete.
Narrative function: long-range bridge toward Consciousness Transfer / Neural Continuity.
Gameplay anchors in part one: evidence, anomalies, Thread resonance, dialogue and faction access.
Hard rule: do not require consciousness-transfer gameplay in part one.

## Choir of the Damned — Contact vs interpretation
Thematic role: competing interpretation of the Thread.
Open questions become deliberate mysteries: what they know, what they misunderstand, why they trust the Thread, internal factions.
Gameplay anchors: discovery, resonance, evidence, combat/encounter consequences where supported.
Hard rule: do not reduce the Choir to an enemy faction.

## Cross-faction rule
Every major faction arc must intersect:
- main story;
- one character arc;
- one gameplay system;
- one other faction;
- one persistent consequence.

## Content status
This document defines architecture and production targets. It does not claim that every listed arc is already implemented in the game.
