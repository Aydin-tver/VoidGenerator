# Wanderers — Mission Chains v0.4

## Vertical Slice: Solaris → Ferrum → Nova

Target:
- 3 stations
- 3 factions
- 3–4 NPCs
- 1 character arc
- 1 faction conflict
- 1 mystery
- 4–6 missions
- 2 meaningful choices
- 2 consequences
- 1 world event
- 1 lore/evidence chain
- 1 equipment gate
- 1 combat encounter
- 1 trade/exploration segment

## Chain MSR-01 — Convoy Irregularity
Gameplay: convoy detection/escort/protection.
Narrative: Lena reports a route anomaly.
Evidence: unexpected/unsigned route correction.
Outcome: protect convoy, fail/lose convoy, or gather enough information for follow-up.

## Chain MSR-02 — Registry Report
Gameplay: docking + evidence/read-state.
Narrative: Tidari/registry requests a report.
Choice:
A. disclose the anomaly;
B. withhold selected information.
Consequences: faction reputation / trust / evidence availability, using registered consequence types only.

## Chain MSR-03 — Ferrum Anomaly
Gameplay: travel + scan/discovery + possible combat.
Narrative: evidence points toward a second anomaly.
Outcome: obtain technical/lore evidence; failure may delay rather than erase the chain.

## Chain MSR-04 — Nova's Interpretation
Gameplay: docking + equipment/progression context.
Narrative: Nova interprets a technical clue without resolving the larger mystery.
Outcome: unlock next investigation gate.

## Chain MSR-05 — SIGMA Signal
Gameplay: Thread resonance / discovery where implemented.
Narrative: first controlled contact with SIGMA-related evidence.
Rule: signal is a clue, not a full lore dump.

## Chain MSR-06 — Cross-Faction Pressure
Gameplay: faction reputation + mission availability.
Narrative: two factions request incompatible actions regarding the same evidence.
Choice becomes the first durable player-position marker.

## Mission design rule
Every major mission must have:
Narrative purpose → gameplay action → evidence/lore → choice or pressure → consequence → follow-up.
