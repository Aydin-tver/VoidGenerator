# Information Gameplay

## Evidence chain

```text
route correction
    ↓
registry inconsistency
    ↓
sensor anomaly
    ↓
cross-source pattern
    ↓
SIGMA fragment
```

## Evidence properties

Every evidence object has:

- source;
- reliability;
- observation;
- interpretation;
- certainty;
- related actors;
- related world state;
- possible contradictions.

## Player knowledge

Do not store only `knows_truth = true`.

Store:
- known;
- suspected;
- believed;
- disproven;
- concealed;
- corroborated.

## Acceptance

Two playthroughs may reach different conclusions while the authoritative world remains consistent.
