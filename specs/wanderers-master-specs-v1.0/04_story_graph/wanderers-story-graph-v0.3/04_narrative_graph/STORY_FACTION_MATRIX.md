## Faction pressure rules
- Every major act must contain at least two active faction interests.
- A faction should never exist only as exposition; it needs a gameplay-relevant objective, resource, access gate, consequence, or conflict.
- Faction presence must be traceable to missions or world events.
- `hidden` and `rumor` are narrative states, not implemented gameplay mechanics.

## Primary thematic conflicts
- OmniCorp ↔ Free Merchants: order / autonomy.
- Tidari ↔ information brokers and authorities: knowledge / ownership.
- Arcani ↔ conscious systems and institutions: experimentation / consent.
- Khadar ↔ frontier interests: survival / control.
- Voidari ↔ established institutions: identity / continuity.
- Choir ↔ institutional narratives: belief / interpretation.
