# Lore Revelation Graph v0.3

## Revelation chain

```text
UNKNOWN
  ↓
SIGNAL / ANOMALY
  ↓
OBSERVATION
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
COMPETING INTERPRETATIONS
  ↓
CORROBORATION / CONTRADICTION
  ↓
REVELATION
  ↓
PLAYER CHOICE
  ↓
CONSEQUENCE
```

## Four permanent questions
- Why does the Registry permit anomalous behavior?
- Why do algorithmic systems appear to know more than the player?
- Why does the Thread react to some intelligent systems?
- Why can the player make decisions outside expected protocols?

## Revelation rule
No major revelation may depend on a single disposable dialogue line. Critical truths require a chain of at least two independent clues, unless deliberately designed as a character's unverified claim.

## Perspective rule
Facts can have:
- objective canonical status;
- faction interpretation;
- character interpretation;
- player hypothesis.

These must not be conflated.
