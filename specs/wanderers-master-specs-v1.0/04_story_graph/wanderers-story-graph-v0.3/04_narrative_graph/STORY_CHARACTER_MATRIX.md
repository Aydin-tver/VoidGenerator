## Character participation rule
Every major character must connect to:
1. at least one faction;
2. at least one main-story question;
3. at least one supported gameplay system;
4. at least one other character;
5. at least one possible consequence.

## Knowledge rule
A character may know something the player does not. That knowledge must be represented through dialogue/evidence gates, not invisible author knowledge.
