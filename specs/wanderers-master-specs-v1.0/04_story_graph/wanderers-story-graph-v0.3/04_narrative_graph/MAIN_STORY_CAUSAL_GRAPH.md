# Main Story Causal Graph v0.3

## Acts

### A0 Arrival
Ordinary work establishes the player, ship, stations, first NPC relationships, and first anomaly.

### A1 Anomalies
Repeated route/scan/convoy anomalies establish that the initial incident is not isolated.

### A2 Contradictions
Official explanations conflict with evidence gathered by the player.

### A3 Algorithm War
Historical and current algorithmic conflicts become materially relevant to missions and factions.

### A4 The Gap
Zazor and related actors provide alternative explanations of identity, systems, and institutional history.

### A5 The Thread
Thread resonance becomes a central unresolved phenomenon rather than background lore.

### A6 Choice
Factions and characters present incompatible courses of action; player decisions define the final trajectory.

### A7 Consequence
Earlier choices, relationships, knowledge, and world consequences determine the final configuration.

## Causal invariant
Every act must answer one earlier question while creating at least one new question or consequence.

## Act transition contract
A transition requires a concrete cause: completed mission/event, discovery, faction state, character state, capability, or world consequence. `chapter += 1` by itself is not a narrative cause.
