# Final Systemic RPG Manifest

Wanderers of the Void should be considered a systemic RPG when:

1. The player can define meaningful goals.
2. Multiple capabilities create different approaches.
3. NPCs remember and pursue their own goals.
4. Factions act on interests rather than reputation alone.
5. Economy creates opportunities and consequences.
6. Exploration produces knowledge and opportunities.
7. World simulation connects systems causally.
8. Main story adapts to player state.
9. Information can be incomplete or contradictory.
10. Failure creates new states.
11. Equipment changes gameplay decisions.
12. Choices propagate into the future.
13. Different builds produce different playthroughs.
14. The world contains enough content to repeatedly exercise these systems.
15. The player can feel that their actions changed the world.

Signature identity:

> Wanderers of the Void is an information-driven systemic space RPG where the player does not merely discover the truth — they construct an understanding of the world and decide what to do with it.
