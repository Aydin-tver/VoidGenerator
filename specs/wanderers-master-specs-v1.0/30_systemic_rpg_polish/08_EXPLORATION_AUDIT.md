# Exploration Audit v1.0

Exploration should answer:

Why should the player look?

Possible motivations:
- rumor;
- signal;
- anomaly;
- economic opportunity;
- faction secret;
- NPC request;
- unexplained event;
- curiosity.

Discovery should produce:
- information;
- item;
- capability;
- route;
- relationship;
- economic opportunity;
- mystery;
- world-state change.

## Red flag

Exploration that gives only credits is insufficient.

Target:
Exploration score >= 8/10.
