# Final RPG Scorecard v1.0

Score every dimension from 0 to 10.

## 0–2

## 3–4
Implemented but shallow.

## 5–6
Functional RPG layer.

## 7–8
Strong systemic RPG.

## 9
Exceptional and consistently reactive.

## 10
Signature systemic experience.
