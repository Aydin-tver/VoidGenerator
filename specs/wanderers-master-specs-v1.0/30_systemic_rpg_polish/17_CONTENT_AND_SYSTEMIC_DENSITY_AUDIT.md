# Content & Systemic Density Audit v1.0

Calculate:

Systemic Density =
meaningful system interactions / relevant content units.

Content Quality =
weighted average of content tiers.

Coverage =
systems exercised / systems required.

Agency Density =
meaningful player decisions / major situations.

Do not optimize these metrics blindly.

Use them to find empty areas.

## Example

100 missions with 80% delivery templates:
low density.

40 missions where most use factions + NPC + economy + exploration + information:
high density.
