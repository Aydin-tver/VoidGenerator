# Consequence Audit v1.0

Classify consequences:

Immediate:
- reward;
- damage;
- relationship;
- information.

Medium:
- mission availability;
- faction access;
- NPC behavior;
- prices;
- security;
- routes.

Long:
- faction strategy;
- station state;
- economic structure;
- ending conditions;
- future story.

## Rule

Important choices should leave traces.

Do not announce every consequence immediately.

Delayed consequences are encouraged when the causal relationship remains understandable.
