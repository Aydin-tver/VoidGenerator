# Quest Diversity Audit v1.0

Measure distribution across:

- investigation;
- exploration;
- trade;
- negotiation;
- escort;
- recovery;
- sabotage;
- information brokerage;
- smuggling;
- faction conflict;
- combat;
- survival;
- technical problem solving.

## Red flags

- delivery mission reskinned;
- combat used as universal solution;
- dialogue used as fake choice;
- investigation reduced to scanning one object;
- economy reduced to buy low/sell high;
- exploration reduced to finding marked locations.

Major missions should average 3+ materially different approaches.
