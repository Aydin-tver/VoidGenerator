# World Reactivity Audit v1.0

Test whether player actions propagate into:

- NPC;
- factions;
- economy;
- routes;
- security;
- stations;
- missions;
- exploration;
- information.

## Required test

Perform a major action.

Observe immediate, medium and long-term consequences.

A world that only changes immediately after a quest is not sufficiently systemic.

Target:
20+ meaningful world-state transitions.
