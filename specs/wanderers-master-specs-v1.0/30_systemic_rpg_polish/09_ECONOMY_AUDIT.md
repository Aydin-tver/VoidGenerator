# Economy Audit v1.0

Verify:

- supply/demand;
- price causality;
- route response;
- shortages;
- surpluses;
- speculation;
- economic information;
- legal/restricted markets;
- faction economic interests;
- NPC economic behavior.

## Tests

1. Can the player profit from knowledge?
2. Can the player change a market?
3. Can the player exploit or create a shortage?
4. Can economy generate missions?
5. Can economic action affect factions/security?

Avoid one dominant trade route.

Target:
Economy score >= 7/10.
