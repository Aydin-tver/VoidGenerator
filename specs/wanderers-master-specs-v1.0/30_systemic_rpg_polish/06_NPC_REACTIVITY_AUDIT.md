# NPC Reactivity Audit v1.0

Every deep recurring NPC should have:

- goal;
- need;
- fear;
- memory;
- beliefs;
- secrets;
- relationships;
- faction position;
- capability;
- future intentions.

## Test

Perform a meaningful action in front of the NPC.

Then revisit the NPC later.

The conversation or behavior should change when relevant.

Target:
- 15+ deep NPC arcs;
- persistent memory;
- relationship variation;
- NPC-generated opportunities;
- NPC/faction conflict.
