# Faction Audit v1.0

For every major faction verify:

- strategic goal;
- resources;
- internal groups;
- economic interests;
- technology;
- secrets;
- NPC representatives;
- relationships with other factions;
- reaction to player actions;
- faction-generated missions;
- privileges;
- obligations.

## Test

Remove reputation numbers from the design.

Would the faction still provide meaningful gameplay?

If not, the faction is too shallow.

Target:
Faction interaction score >= 8/10.
