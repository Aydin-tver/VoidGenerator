# Economy Telemetry & Balancing v1.0

Track:

- average price;
- price volatility;
- supply/demand ratio;
- player profit;
- profit by activity;
- route profitability;
- cargo utilization;
- shortage duration;
- surplus duration;
- smuggling success;
- inspection rate;
- economic opportunity frequency;
- player capital concentration.

Warning signs:
- one dominant trade route;
- infinite low-risk profit;
- permanent shortages;
- prices oscillating wildly;
- smuggling always optimal;
- exploration economically irrelevant;
- combat never affects economy.

Economy should create choices, not a single optimal strategy.
