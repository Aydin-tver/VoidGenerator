# Trade Routes v1.0

Routes connect economic nodes.

Route value depends on:
- price differential;
- cargo volume;
- travel distance;
- risk;
- security;
- piracy;
- faction restrictions;
- demand stability;
- special events.

The optimal route should not always be obvious.

A dangerous route can offer:
- higher margin;
- rare goods;
- restricted cargo;
- time advantage.

Player can discover routes rather than receiving every opportunity from the UI.
