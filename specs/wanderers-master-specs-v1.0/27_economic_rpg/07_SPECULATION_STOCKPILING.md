# Speculation & Stockpiling v1.0

Players can anticipate future shortages.

Examples:
- buy reactor components before a refinery shutdown;
- stockpile medical goods before a station crisis;
- preserve rare technology for a faction conflict.

Risks:
- capital locked in cargo;
- prices may fall;
- cargo space;
- piracy;
- storage cost;
- legal exposure;
- faction response.

The player should be able to make a calculated economic bet.
