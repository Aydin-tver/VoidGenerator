# Goods & Market Types v1.0

Goods should have different economic roles.

## Categories

- basic supplies;
- industrial materials;
- ship components;
- fuel/energy;
- medical goods;
- luxury goods;
- strategic resources;
- technology;
- restricted goods;
- information.

## Market properties

Each good can have:
- perishability;
- legality;
- scarcity;
- weight/volume;
- strategic importance;
- faction sensitivity;
- production difficulty;
- regional specialization.

Not every good needs a complex simulation.

Prioritize goods that create gameplay.
