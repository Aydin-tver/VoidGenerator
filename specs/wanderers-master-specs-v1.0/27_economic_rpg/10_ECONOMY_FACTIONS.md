# Economy × Factions v1.0

Factions have economic interests.

They can:
- control supply;
- subsidize routes;
- impose restrictions;
- buy strategic resources;
- manipulate scarcity;
- protect trade;
- sabotage competitors;
- create procurement demand.

Internal faction groups can disagree.

Example:

OmniCorp Research wants cheap components.
OmniCorp Security wants restricted access.
OmniCorp Expansion wants market dominance.

The player can exploit these internal conflicts.
