# Economic Opportunities v1.0

Opportunities emerge from state.

Examples:

Shortage
→ high price
→ profitable delivery.

Surplus
→ low price
→ cheap acquisition.

Route disruption
→ alternative route premium.

Faction conflict
→ restricted goods demand.

Station damage
→ repair component demand.

Discovery
→ new resource access.

The system should be able to generate opportunities without authoring a bespoke quest for every case.
