# Economic Build Archetypes v1.0

## Trader
Optimizes legal price spreads.

## Broker
Trades information and relationships.

## Smuggler
Accepts legal/security risk for higher margins.

## Salvager
Finds low-cost goods through exploration.

## Logistics Specialist
Uses cargo/route efficiency.

## Faction Supplier
Optimizes strategic contracts.

## Speculator
Uses knowledge to anticipate market shifts.

Build identity should come from decisions and capabilities, not only passive bonuses.
