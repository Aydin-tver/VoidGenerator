# Economic Information Gameplay v1.0

Information is an economic asset.

The player may know:
- future shortage;
- hidden supplier;
- unusual demand;
- faction procurement plan;
- route risk;
- secret stockpile.

Player can:
- exploit it;
- sell it;
- trade it;
- conceal it;
- disclose it;
- verify it first.

Information should have:
- reliability;
- age;
- source;
- value;
- affected markets;
- affected actors.

This connects Economic RPG with the game's unique Information Gameplay identity.
