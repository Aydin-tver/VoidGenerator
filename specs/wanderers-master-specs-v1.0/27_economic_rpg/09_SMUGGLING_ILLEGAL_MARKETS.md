# Smuggling & Illegal Markets v1.0

Some goods have legal restrictions.

Player options:
- legal trade;
- licensed transport;
- covert transport;
- bribery/negotiation;
- information brokerage;
- hidden storage.

Risk dimensions:
- inspection;
- faction suspicion;
- cargo seizure;
- reputation;
- fines;
- relationship damage;
- route restrictions.

Covert equipment and skills can change the risk profile.

Smuggling should be a systemic alternative, not a single quest mechanic.
