# Stage 27 Vertical Slice — Ferrum Reactor Components

## Initial state

Ferrum has:
- stable reactor component supply;
- moderate demand;
- normal security.

## Event

A refinery suffers an infrastructure problem.

Production falls by 50%.

## Market response

- component stock declines;
- prices rise;
- repair demand increases;
- nearby stations become attractive suppliers.

## Player options

### 1. Buy early
Stockpile before the price peak.

### 2. Deliver components
Profit from shortage.

### 3. Find another supplier
Use exploration/NPC/faction information.

### 4. Negotiate
Obtain privileged access.

### 5. Smuggle restricted components
Higher margin, higher risk.

### 6. Withhold cargo
Keep the components for a future opportunity.

### 7. Sell information
Tell a merchant or faction about the shortage.

## Consequences

Different actions alter:
- prices;
- station supply;
- faction relationships;
- NPC goals;
- security;
- future missions.

The vertical slice must demonstrate that the economy creates gameplay without requiring a fixed quest chain.
