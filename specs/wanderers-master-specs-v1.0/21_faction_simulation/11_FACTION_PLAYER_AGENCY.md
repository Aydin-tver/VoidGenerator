# Faction Gameplay & Player Agency v1.0

The player should be able to pursue faction relationships intentionally.

Possible goals:
- rise inside a faction;
- remain independent;
- play factions against each other;
- obtain restricted technology;
- protect a faction;
- undermine a faction;
- expose a faction;
- exploit economic differences;
- broker peace;
- profit from conflict.

There should be no mandatory single faction alignment.

A player can maintain mixed relationships.
