# Faction Secrets & Intelligence v1.0

Faction knowledge is an asset.

Secrets may concern:
- illegal operations;
- technology;
- financial arrangements;
- historical events;
- covert alliances;
- manipulated records;
- internal power struggles.

Player can:
- discover;
- verify;
- trade;
- reveal;
- conceal;
- weaponize;
- misunderstand.

Important:
Knowing a secret is not equivalent to automatically knowing its meaning.

Evidence quality matters.
