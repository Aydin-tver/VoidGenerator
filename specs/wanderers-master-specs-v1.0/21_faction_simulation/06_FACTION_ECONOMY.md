# Faction Economy v1.0

Factions have economic interests.

Track:
- supply;
- demand;
- strategic goods;
- routes;
- production;
- shortages;
- logistics;
- protected markets.

Player actions can affect these variables.

Example:

Player repeatedly supplies reactor components to Ferrum.

Result:
- Ferrum shortage decreases;
- local prices change;
- Engineer Nova's mission pool changes;
- OmniCorp loses a potential supplier;
- Free Merchants gain influence.

Economy becomes a source of faction gameplay.
