# Faction Technology v1.0

Technology is part of faction identity.

Each faction may have:
- standard equipment;
- restricted equipment;
- prototypes;
- signature technology;
- research projects.

Access can depend on:
- rank;
- trust;
- mission history;
- skills;
- resources;
- discovered secrets.

Technology can create political consequences.

Example:
A player acquires a restricted OmniCorp signal analyzer.

Possible reactions:
- OmniCorp wants it returned;
- rival faction wants a copy;
- researcher offers protection;
- black market offers high payment;
- player can keep it hidden.
