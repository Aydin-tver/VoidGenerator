# Faction Ranks & Privileges v1.0

Reputation should unlock meaningful gameplay.

Example progression:

0 Outsider
1 Contact
2 Associate
3 Member
4 Specialist
5 Trusted Agent

Privileges may include:
- restricted trade;
- repair discounts;
- faction equipment;
- protected docking;
- classified information;
- mission priority;
- access to faction markets;
- access to faction technology;
- emergency assistance.

Higher rank must also create obligations and expectations.

Example:
Trusted Agent:
+ prototype equipment
+ classified missions
+ emergency support

but:
- faction expects loyalty;
- rival factions react;
- refusing critical missions can damage trust.
