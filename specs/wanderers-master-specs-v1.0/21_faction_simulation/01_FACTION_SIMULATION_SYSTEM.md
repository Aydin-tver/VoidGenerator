# Wanderers of the Void — Faction Simulation System v1.0

## Goal

Turn factions from narrative labels into systemic actors.

A faction must have:
- goals;
- resources;
- internal interests;
- ranks;
- privileges;
- technology;
- economic interests;
- secrets;
- relationships with other factions;
- reactions to player actions;
- missions generated from actual needs.

Core loop:

PLAYER ACTION
→ FACTION STATE CHANGE
→ INTERNAL / EXTERNAL REACTION
→ NEW OPPORTUNITY OR CONFLICT
→ PLAYER CHOICE
→ CONSEQUENCE

## Anti-cosmetic rule

Faction reputation alone is insufficient.

Two players with equal reputation may have very different relationships with a faction because they:
- helped different internal groups;
- exposed different secrets;
- damaged different assets;
- acquired different faction technology;
- became associated with different rivals.
