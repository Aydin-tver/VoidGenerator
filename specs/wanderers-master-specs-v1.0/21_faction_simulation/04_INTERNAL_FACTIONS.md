# Internal Faction Groups v1.0

Major factions should contain competing interests.

Examples:

## OmniCorp
- Expansion Directorate
- Security Directorate
- Research Directorate

## Free Merchants
- Independent Traders
- Convoy Union
- Risk-Takers

## Tidari
- Registry Administration
- Enforcement Office
- Archival Directorate

## Arcani
- Traditionalists
- Researchers
- Isolationists

Internal groups can:
- offer different missions;
- disagree about player actions;
- compete for influence;
- leak information;
- reward different builds.

The player does not need to formally join every internal group.
Association itself can have consequences.
