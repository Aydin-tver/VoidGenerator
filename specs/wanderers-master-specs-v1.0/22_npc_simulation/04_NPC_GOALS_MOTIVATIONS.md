# NPC Goals & Motivations v1.0

NPC goals should explain behavior.

Goal types:
- survival;
- wealth;
- career;
- loyalty;
- research;
- revenge;
- protection;
- independence;
- discovery;
- ideology;
- relationship;
- secrecy.

Each important NPC should have:

Primary goal:
what they are actively trying to achieve.

Secondary goals:
what they would pursue if circumstances change.

Constraints:
what they refuse to do.

Fears:
what they try to avoid.

Leverage:
what other actors can use against them.

## Goal conflict

NPCs should sometimes have incompatible goals.

Example:

Nova wants:
- protect Ferrum engineers;
- discover the anomaly;
- preserve her position.

If research requires exposing Ferrum to risk, she must choose.

The player can influence that choice.
