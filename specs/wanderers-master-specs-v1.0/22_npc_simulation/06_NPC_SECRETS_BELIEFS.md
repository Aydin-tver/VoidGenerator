# NPC Secrets & Beliefs v1.0

NPC knowledge is partial.

For each important fact, an NPC may:
- know;
- suspect;
- believe incorrectly;
- deny;
- hide;
- misunderstand.

Example:

Nova:
- knows Ferrum anomaly exists;
- suspects someone manipulated the telemetry;
- does not know who;
- believes OmniCorp is probably involved;
- hides one earlier scan because it could damage her career.

This prevents omniscient NPC dialogue.
