# NPC × Skills × Equipment v1.0

NPC reactions can depend on demonstrated capability.

Examples:

Engineering:
NPC asks the player to diagnose a reactor.

Sensors:
NPC trusts the player's anomaly analysis.

Commerce:
NPC offers a trade contract.

Social:
NPC reveals information after successful negotiation.

Covert:
NPC may never know the player acquired evidence.

Equipment can also alter reactions.

A faction prototype may:
- impress a researcher;
- alarm security;
- attract a black-market broker.

Skills determine how the player uses the capability.
Equipment determines what capability is available.
