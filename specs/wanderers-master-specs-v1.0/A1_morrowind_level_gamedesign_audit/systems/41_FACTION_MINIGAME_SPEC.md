# Faction Mini-Game Requirement

Every major faction needs its own gameplay identity.

Examples:

Free Merchants:
- trade routes;
- convoy protection;
- market intelligence;
- independent contracts.

Tidari:
- information;
- registry access;
- records;
- data disputes.

OmniCorp:
- logistics;
- contracts;
- controlled access;
- industrial technology.

Arcani:
- experiments;
- anomalies;
- research choices;
- ethical conflicts.

Khadar:
- survival logistics;
- dangerous regions;
- resource scarcity.

Voidari:
- identity/consciousness research;
- hidden knowledge;
- high-risk discoveries.

Choir:
- resonance interpretation;
- signal discovery;
- communication uncertainty.

These are design directions; they must only use capabilities the game can
actually implement.
