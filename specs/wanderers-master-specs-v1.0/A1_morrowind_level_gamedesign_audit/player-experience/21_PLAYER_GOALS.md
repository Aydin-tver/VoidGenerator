# Player Self-Directed Goals

The game should support goals that are not explicit mission objectives.

Examples:

- Become trusted by Free Merchants.
- Discover why a convoy route was altered.
- Find a lost ship.
- Become wealthy through a trade route.
- Obtain a unique reactor.
- Investigate the Tidari archives.
- Avoid OmniCorp attention.
- Determine whether SIGMA is an AI, signal or something else.
- Learn what the player's own memory is hiding.
- Build a relationship with a specific NPC.

The system should provide information and opportunities, not force one path.
