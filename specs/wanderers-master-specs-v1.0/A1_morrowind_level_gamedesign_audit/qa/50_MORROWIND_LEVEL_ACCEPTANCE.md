# Morrowind-Level Acceptance Criteria

Do not declare the RPG layer complete because:
- lore bible exists;
- mission JSON validates;
- dialogue works;
- branches exist.

Declare a sector RPG-complete only when a playtester can demonstrate:

1. They can ignore the main story for several hours.
2. They can pursue a faction-driven goal.
3. They can discover content from information rather than markers.
4. Their choices change later content.
5. NPCs remember meaningful actions.
6. At least two factions respond differently to player history.
7. Equipment opens at least some narrative opportunities.
8. Revisiting a station reveals changed context.
9. They can miss major information and still continue.
10. Two playthroughs produce materially different contexts.
11. There are secrets not required by the main quest.
12. The world can be understood through play, not only exposition.

This is the real acceptance test.
