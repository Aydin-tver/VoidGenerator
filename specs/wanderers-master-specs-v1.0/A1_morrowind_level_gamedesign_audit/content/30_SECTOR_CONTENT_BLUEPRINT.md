# Sector-Level Content Blueprint

A target production sector should contain:

## 6 major stations

Each:
- unique visual identity;
- dominant faction;
- secondary faction;
- 3–6 local stories;
- 2–4 recurring NPCs;
- rumors;
- secrets;
- faction conflict;
- economic identity.

## 3 major faction arcs

Each:
- 8–12 missions;
- rank/progression;
- exclusive knowledge;
- exclusive reward;
- rival;
- internal conflict.

## 15 recurring NPCs

Each with:
- goal;
- secret;
- relationship network;
- memory state;
- at least one personal arc.

## 30+ discoveries

Mix:
- wrecks;
- signals;
- records;
- hidden routes;
- anomalies;
- abandoned installations;
- faction secrets.

## 50+ evidence/lore nodes

At least 10 should be cross-connected to multiple stories.
