# 04 — Faction Gameplay Gap

Seven factions are defined narratively, but they must become seven distinct
gameplay/social frameworks.

For every major faction implement:

```text
identity
↓
entry requirements
↓
initial contact
↓
trust/reputation
↓
rank/progression
↓
exclusive knowledge
↓
exclusive missions
↓
exclusive rewards
↓
rivals
↓
conflicts
↓
world consequences
```

## Minimum target

Each major faction needs:

- 8–12 meaningful missions;
- 3–5 recurring NPCs;
- 2–3 internal conflicts;
- 2 rival-faction conflicts;
- faction-specific rewards;
- at least one exclusive information chain;
- at least one mutually incompatible choice;
- visible consequences.

Not every faction needs equal volume, but every major faction must have
a distinct reason to exist as a gameplay path.
