# 07 — Exploration & Discovery Gap

No GPS is good, but removing a marker is not automatically exploration.

Target:

```text
rumor
 ↓
partial information
 ↓
navigation
 ↓
risk
 ↓
discovery
 ↓
interpretation
 ↓
new information
```

## Space-specific equivalent of Morrowind exploration

Instead of caves and ruins, Wanderers should use:

- unregistered signals;
- abandoned ships;
- altered routes;
- dead stations;
- salvage fields;
- old navigation records;
- faction checkpoints;
- anomalous regions;
- hidden trade routes;
- contradictory registry data;
- rare encounters;
- coordinates learned from NPCs;
- locations revealed only by evidence.

Each discovery should ideally have:
- lore;
- gameplay;
- reward;
- future consequence;
- optionality.

Exploration must produce knowledge, not only credits.
