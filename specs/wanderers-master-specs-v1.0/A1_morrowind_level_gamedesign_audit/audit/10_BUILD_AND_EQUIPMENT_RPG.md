# 10 — Build / Equipment RPG Gap

Equipment currently risks being primarily numerical progression.

Target:

```text
equipment
 ↓
capability
 ↓
new possibility
 ↓
new narrative option
```

Examples:

- stronger scanner → discover otherwise invisible anomaly;
- cargo capacity → accept a high-risk trade;
- stealth capability → avoid a faction checkpoint;
- reactor capacity → sustain unusual route;
- weapon capability → survive optional dangerous location;
- specialized module → interact with a unique event.

The rule:

> Equipment should sometimes change what story the player can access,
> not only how efficiently they complete it.
