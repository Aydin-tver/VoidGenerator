# 12 — Replayability

A deep RPG should generate materially different stories across runs.

Wanderers can achieve this through:

- faction alignment;
- information choices;
- NPC trust;
- evidence discovered/missed;
- equipment-based opportunities;
- station consequences;
- optional missions;
- hidden identity discoveries.

Target:

Two players completing the main story should be able to possess different:
- allies;
- evidence;
- faction relationships;
- unlocked missions;
- unresolved mysteries;
- station states;
- final interpretation of events.

They do not need completely different campaigns.
Contextual divergence is enough.
