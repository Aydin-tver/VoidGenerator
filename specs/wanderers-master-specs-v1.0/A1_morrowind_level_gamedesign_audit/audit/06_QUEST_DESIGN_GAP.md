# 06 — Quest Design Gap

The current MSR chain is a good investigation prototype but too linear to
represent a full RPG.

Target quest families:

1. Main mystery
2. Faction
3. Character
4. Station/local
5. Trade/economy
6. Exploration
7. Combat
8. Escort/convoy
9. Salvage
10. Investigation
11. Moral/strategic choice
12. Rumor/secret
13. Multi-faction conflict
14. Equipment-gated opportunity
15. World-event response

## Quest quality test

A strong quest should answer:

- What does the player want?
- What does the issuer want?
- What does the player know?
- What is hidden?
- What can go wrong?
- What can the player choose?
- What gameplay action expresses that choice?
- What changes afterwards?
- Who remembers?
- What future content changes?

If a quest is only:

```text
fly → click → text → reward
```

it is not sufficient for the target RPG depth.
