# 02 — What "Morrowind-Level" Means for This Project

The comparison should not mean reproducing Morrowind's fantasy setting or old
mechanics.

Relevant design properties are:

1. The world is worth learning.
2. Factions represent different interests, not just quest providers.
3. Faction progression has requirements, rank, rewards and conflicts.
4. Exploration can generate meaningful discoveries without a quest marker.
5. NPC information is a gameplay resource.
6. Side content can reveal world structure.
7. Actions can close or open future possibilities.
8. Revisiting locations can have changed meaning.
9. The player can pursue self-created goals.
10. Lore is delivered through gameplay, not only exposition.
