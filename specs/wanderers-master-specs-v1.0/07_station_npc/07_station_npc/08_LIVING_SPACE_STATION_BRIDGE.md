# Living Space ↔ Station Narrative Bridge v0.6

## Purpose
Make the player's ship/living space a continuation of station RPG content.

## Supported content
The living space can surface:
- messages;
- journal updates;
- evidence review;
- character communications;
- mission decisions;
- equipment context;
- identity/reflection scenes.

## Example

Station:
Lena asks the player to keep a route correction confidential.

Living Space:
The player receives a message from the registry asking for clarification.

The player can review:
- Lena's request;
- registry evidence;
- previous decision;
- available response.

The actual consequence remains owned by the narrative/domain state.

## No invented mechanics
Do not require:
- walking inside the ship;
- furniture interactions;
- simulated crew schedules;
- physical message terminals.

Unless these are later implemented.
