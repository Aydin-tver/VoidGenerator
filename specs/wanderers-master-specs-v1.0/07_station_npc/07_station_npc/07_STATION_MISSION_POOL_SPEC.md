# Station Mission Pool Specification v0.6

## Mission categories

### Normal
Trade, delivery, salvage and ordinary station work.

### Character
Advances a named NPC relationship/arc.

### Faction
Represents a faction goal/conflict.

### Investigative
Acquires evidence or resolves a contradiction.

### World event
Responds to temporary world conditions.

### Main story
Advances the central narrative.

## Station pool rules

Every station should contain:
- 2–4 normal activities;
- 1–2 recurring character hooks;
- 1 faction hook where appropriate;
- 1 investigative hook during relevant story acts;
- 0–1 main-story mission at a time;
- world-event missions only when triggered.

## Avoid
- identical delivery missions with different text;
- NPCs giving lore monologues;
- mission markers replacing information;
- missions with no gameplay verification;
- rewards unrelated to the mission's narrative purpose.
