# Faction Master v1

The seven-faction structure below follows the existing RPG master plan. Internal details are design direction until verified against the current canon files.

| Faction | Core tension | Narrative role |
|---|---|---|
| OmniCorp | order vs autonomy | institutional power, security, control |
| Free Merchants | freedom vs survival | trade, independence, practical frontier life |
| Tidari / Registry | information vs ownership | archives, identity, records, information control |
| Arcani | experimentation vs consent | science, technology, ethical boundaries |
| Khadar | frontier power / survival | force, territory, pragmatic conflict |
| Voidari | continuity vs identity boundaries | hidden long-term existential layer |
| Choir | belief / interpretation vs evidence | social response to the Thread and hidden truths |

## Required faction model
Each faction must define:
- public identity;
- true/strategic goals;
- resources;
- economic role;
- military/security role;
- relationship to AI;
- relationship to Thread;
- relationship to Nexite;
- internal factions;
- external allies/enemies;
- fears;
- secrets;
- misconceptions;
- what it wants from the player;
- what it can offer;
- what the player can change.

## Anti-monoculture rule
No faction is universally correct or universally evil. A faction may be right about one problem and dangerously wrong about another.
