# Character Master v1

## Character architecture
Major characters must connect to at least:
`1 faction + 1 story question + 1 gameplay system + 1 other character + 1 potential consequence`.

## Character state
Every major character gets:
- trust/relationship state;
- known facts;
- secrets exposed;
- promises/debts;
- relevant faction alignment;
- arc stage;
- memories/previous encounters where supported.

## No teleporting characters
A character's presence must be consistent with the station/world model. If the game cannot simulate movement, narrative presence should be represented as communication/message/context rather than silently teleporting the NPC.
