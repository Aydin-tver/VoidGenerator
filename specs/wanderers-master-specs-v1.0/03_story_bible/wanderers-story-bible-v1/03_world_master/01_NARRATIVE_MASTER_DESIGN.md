# Wanderers — Narrative Master Design v1.0

## Status
Production design foundation. Existing canon is preserved where known; proposed material is explicitly marked `DESIGN PROPOSAL` until promoted to canon.

## Core premise
Wanderers is an RPG about autonomy, information, identity and the consequences of intelligent systems making choices inside a fragmented frontier.

The player is not a chosen biological hero. The current master direction treats the player-facing protagonist as a ship/shipboard AI whose agency gradually becomes a central mystery. This should be treated as the intended narrative direction and reconciled with the actual player-state implementation before content lock.

## Three narrative layers
1. **Personal:** Who am I, and why can I make decisions outside expected protocols?
2. **Political:** Who should control information, trade, AI autonomy and dangerous technology?
3. **Existential:** What is the Thread, and why does it react to conscious systems?

## Central progression
`ordinary work → anomaly → contradiction → faction conflict → algorithmic conflict → SIGMA → Zazor/Voidari traces → Thread → identity → choice`

The player should discover this structure indirectly rather than receive it as exposition.

## Four persistent questions
- Why does the Registry permit anomalies?
- Why do algorithmic systems appear to know more than the player?
- Why does the Thread react to some AIs?
- Why can the player make decisions not predicted by the original protocol?

## Narrative pillars
- Lore is discovered through play, not only read.
- Missions create state, not only rewards.
- Factions have competing goals and internal disagreements.
- NPCs are attached to places and conflicts.
- Choices need persistent representation when they matter later.
- No mandatory GPS/quest-pointer solution; navigation remains knowledge-based.
- No unsupported mechanics are assumed.

## Scale target
The intended depth is Morrowind-like in **density and interconnection**, not in world simulation scope: many interlocking factions, characters, mysteries, histories, optional discoveries and consequences, implemented through Wanderers' actual gameplay loop.

## Forbidden narrative shortcuts
- moral alignment bar;
- text-only choices presented as major consequences without state;
- arbitrary hacking/diplomacy/social mechanics unless implemented;
- NPC schedules or physical station walking unless implemented;
- world simulation hidden behind prose;
- quest chains that exist only to deliver lore paragraphs.
