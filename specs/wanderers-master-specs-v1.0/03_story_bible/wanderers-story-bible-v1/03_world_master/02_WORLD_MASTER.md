# World Master v1

## Scope
This document defines the shared world model required before large-scale quest authoring.

## World structure
The frontier is a network of stations, regions, routes, trade relationships and competing information systems. Stations are narrative anchors: each major station should have an economic identity, faction presence, local problem, recurring characters, rumors and at least one story/mystery hook.

## Known thematic technologies / concepts
- **Thread / Нить:** a major unresolved phenomenon; its deeper nature is intentionally revealed late.
- **Nexite:** strategic technology/material tied to progression and larger conflicts; exact canon properties must remain synchronized with the existing lore bible.
- **SIGMA:** algorithmic intelligence/entity that should act as a narrative agent with goals, knowledge, unknowns and a relationship to the player.
- **KarmaCore / Mitra:** major actors in the historical/current algorithmic conflict.
- **Zazor / Зазор:** frontier space associated with alternative identity, informal systems and hidden infrastructure.
- **Voidari / Войдари:** foundational precursor/hidden actor for the later consciousness-transfer storyline.
- **Consciousness Transfer / Neural Continuity Protocol:** proposed/established long-range lore direction: transfer of personality structure among biological brain, AI, digital carrier and Thread node, with the dangerous possibility of mutual influence.

## Canon discipline
Every important world fact must have:
`stable ID → canonical statement → source → who knows → first reveal → contradictions/rumors → status`.

Possible statuses: `CANON`, `PARTIAL`, `RUMOR`, `FALSE_IN_WORLD`, `PLAYER_DISCOVERY`, `UNKNOWN`.

## Station principle
A station is not a second game mode. It is a narrative wrapper around existing systems: docking, trade, mission access, dialogue, ship progression and information.
