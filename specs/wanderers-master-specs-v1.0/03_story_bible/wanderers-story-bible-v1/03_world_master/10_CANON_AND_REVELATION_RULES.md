# Canon and Revelation Rules v1

## Truth layers
1. **Authoritative truth** — what actually exists in canon.
2. **World knowledge** — what a faction/NPC believes.
3. **Player knowledge** — what the player has discovered.
4. **Theory** — a player interpretation not yet confirmed.

Never collapse these into one boolean `knows_secret` unless the content truly has only one meaningful state.

## Revelation rules
- Early game answers practical questions and creates deeper ones.
- Major revelations require setup.
- A revelation should recontextualize earlier evidence, not merely add a new paragraph.
- Contradictions are intentional only when they are owned by a character/faction perspective or are a mystery state.
- Canon changes require updating dependent missions, dialogue, evidence and terminology.

## Future-content rule
Voidari/Consciousness Transfer can be seeded early, but its complete mechanism should not become common knowledge before the narrative reaches the appropriate late-game revelation.
