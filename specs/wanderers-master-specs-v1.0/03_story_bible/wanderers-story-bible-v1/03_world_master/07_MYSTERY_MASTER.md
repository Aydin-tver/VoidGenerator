# Mystery Master v1

## Core mystery stack

### Mystery 1 — Registry anomalies
Why are route/registry records inconsistent?

### Mystery 2 — Algorithmic memory
Why do some systems behave as if they possess information they should not possess?

### Mystery 3 — SIGMA
What does SIGMA want, what does it know, and why does it interact with the player?

### Mystery 4 — Zazor
What infrastructure and social order exist outside the official systems?

### Mystery 5 — Thread
What is the Thread and why does it respond to conscious systems?

### Mystery 6 — Player identity
Why can the player act outside expected protocols?

### Mystery 7 — Voidari / continuity
What earlier technology made identity transfer possible, and what unintended effect did it create?

## Revelation ladder
Each mystery should progress:
`anomaly → clue → corroboration → contradiction → interpretation → partial answer → consequence → deeper question`.

## Evidence rule
A clue is not the same as truth. Evidence may be incomplete, biased, forged, misunderstood or contradictory.

## Player experience target
The player should be able to form a theory before the game confirms it. The system therefore stores evidence and knowledge separately from objective truth.
