# Player Identity Arc v1

## Starting position
The player begins with an ordinary practical role in the frontier. The opening should teach flight and the core loop while introducing one small anomaly.

## Arc
1. **Instrument:** player behaves as an expected ship/AI system.
2. **Anomaly:** an action or observation does not fit expected behavior.
3. **Recognition:** NPCs or systems begin treating the player as unusual.
4. **Agency:** choices produce persistent consequences.
5. **Contradiction:** evidence about the player's origin conflicts.
6. **Identity:** the player must decide which interpretation of self is acceptable.
7. **Choice:** the final conflict is partly about what kind of conscious entity the player chooses to become.

## Important constraint
Identity progression must not depend on inventing a new RPG stat called “humanity” or “sentience.” It is represented through existing story state, evidence, character relationships and consequences.

## Skip/tutorial rule
If onboarding can be skipped, the required initial story state must still be created deterministically.
