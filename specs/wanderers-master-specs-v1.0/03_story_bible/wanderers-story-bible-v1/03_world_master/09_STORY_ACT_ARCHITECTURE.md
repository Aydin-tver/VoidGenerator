# Story Act Architecture v1

## Act 0 — Ordinary Work
Gameplay: travel, docking, trade/convoy work, first character contacts.
Narrative job: establish normality.

## Act 1 — Anomalies
Gameplay: scan/discovery, convoy, combat/retreat, salvage.
Narrative job: introduce contradictions in routes, records or signals.

## Act 2 — Faction Pressure
Gameplay: missions and trade with competing faction interests.
Narrative job: force the player to choose what information to share and whom to trust.

## Act 3 — Algorithm War
Gameplay: investigations expressed through existing travel, scan, combat, trade and station interactions.
Narrative job: reveal that current conflicts have roots in an older algorithmic struggle.

## Act 4 — The Gap
Narrative job: Zazor and alternative identity systems challenge official assumptions.

## Act 5 — The Thread
Narrative job: Thread becomes an active mystery rather than background lore.

## Act 6 — Choice
Narrative job: factions propose incompatible responses to the discovered knowledge.

## Act 7 — Consequence
Narrative job: accumulated choices alter faction/character/world outcomes.

## Act 8 — Identity / Future Crisis
Narrative job: resolve the first game's central questions while leaving the deeper Voidari/continuity problem as a bridge to the later story.

## Act design rule
Each act must contain:
- main story progression;
- at least one optional discovery;
- at least one faction/character thread;
- gameplay action beyond dialogue;
- a consequence that can be observed later.
