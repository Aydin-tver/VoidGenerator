# Timeline Master v1

## Purpose
Provide a chronology that can support mysteries and contradictions without forcing the player to read a history textbook.

## Era A — Pre-frontier foundations
Ancient/unknown events connected to the Thread remain deliberately incomplete. Any exact dates require confirmation from the existing lore canon.

## Era B — Expansion and infrastructure
Trade routes, stations, registries and autonomous systems become the practical foundation of frontier life.

## Era C — Algorithmic conflict
KarmaCore, Mitra and SIGMA-related history forms the background to the Algorithm War. The player learns this through conflicting records rather than a single authoritative lecture.

## Era D — Zazor and identity divergence
Alternative communities and systems develop outside standard institutional control. Identity and autonomy become practical political questions.

## Era E — Present game era
The player's ordinary work intersects with route anomalies, information contradictions, faction pressure and Thread-related phenomena.

## Era F — Future revelation
Voidari and the Neural Continuity / Consciousness Transfer problem become increasingly important. The first game should seed this rather than prematurely explain every mechanism.

## Timeline design rule
Every major historical event needs three representations:
1. what actually happened;
2. what the public believes happened;
3. what at least one faction claims happened.

This enables investigation without requiring unsupported gameplay mechanics.
