# Quest Capability Routing v1.0

Capabilities come from:

- skills;
- equipment;
- faction access;
- NPC relationships;
- knowledge;
- economy.

Example:

A locked technical anomaly can be approached through:

Engineering:
repair and inspect.

Sensors:
scan remotely.

Covert:
approach without triggering security.

Social:
obtain authorization.

Commerce:
hire a specialist.

Combat:
force access.

Not every quest needs every approach.

The author must explicitly identify supported approaches.
