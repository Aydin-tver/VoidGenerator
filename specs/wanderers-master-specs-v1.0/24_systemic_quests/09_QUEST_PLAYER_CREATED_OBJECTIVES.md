# Player-Created Quest Objectives v1.0

The player should sometimes define the objective.

Examples:

- "I want to find out who changed the route."
- "I want to protect Nova."
- "I want to profit from this shortage."
- "I want to stay independent from OmniCorp."
- "I want to acquire this technology."

The game can respond through:
- information;
- NPCs;
- missions;
- faction opportunities;
- economic opportunities.

This is essential for agency.

Not every objective needs a formal quest marker.
