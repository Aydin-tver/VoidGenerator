# Stage 24 Vertical Slice

## Quest: Ferrum Anomaly Investigation

### Player problem

Determine what is causing abnormal Ferrum telemetry while deciding whom to trust.

### Approach 1 — Technical investigation
Engineering + scanner.

Result:
technical evidence.

### Approach 2 — Information network
Talk to Nova + Tidari.

Result:
conflicting testimony.

### Approach 3 — Covert investigation
Silent Drive + covert skill.

Result:
observe restricted activity.

### Approach 4 — Faction leverage
Use OmniCorp / Free Merchant relationships.

Result:
access or pressure.

### Approach 5 — Economic investigation
Follow suspicious cargo and route transactions.

Result:
commercial evidence.

### Approach 6 — Direct action
Confront suspected actor.

Result:
fast resolution with high risk.

### Failure

Evidence lost or suspect escapes.

But:
new witness / damaged hardware / faction reaction creates recovery path.

### Partial success

Cause identified but responsible actor unknown.

### Success

Cause identified and evidence secured.

### Exceptional

Cause + network + faction implications identified.

The mission must feel different depending on player build and prior choices.
