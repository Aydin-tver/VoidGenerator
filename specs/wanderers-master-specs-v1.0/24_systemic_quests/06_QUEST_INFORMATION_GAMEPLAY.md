# Quest Information Gameplay v1.0

Information is a resource.

Player may need to:
- discover;
- compare;
- verify;
- interpret;
- trade;
- conceal.

A quest can be easier with better information.

Example:

Without evidence:
three plausible suspects.

With registry data:
one suspect becomes more likely.

With scanner evidence:
a second contradiction appears.

With NPC testimony:
a new faction connection emerges.

The player decides what to believe.
