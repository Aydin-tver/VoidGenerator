## Acceptance gate

Stage 24 passes when:
- major quests have 3+ approaches;
- non-combat approaches exist;
- failure creates state;
- partial success exists;
- consequences propagate;
- player goals can differ from quest author intent;
- branches can converge;
- dynamic missions have world causes;
- no major quest requires a single hidden correct sequence.
