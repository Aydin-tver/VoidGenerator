# Non-Quest Discovery Rules v1.0

A discovery does not require:

- active quest;
- objective marker;
- destination marker;
- explicit instruction.

It may begin from:
- an unusual signal;
- a rumor;
- a strange price;
- a suspicious convoy;
- an unexplained object;
- an NPC comment.

The player can create their own objective:

"I want to find out why this ship is broadcasting an old registry code."

The system may then create opportunities without turning the discovery into a traditional quest immediately.
