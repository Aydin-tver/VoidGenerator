# Discovery Types v1.0

## 1. Environmental anomaly
Unexpected signal, radiation, route behavior or debris.

## 2. Abandoned object
Wreck, station module, cargo container, probe.

## 3. Hidden location
Site that is not part of the normal travel flow.

## 4. Secret route
A route discovered through information or observation.

## 5. NPC lead
Rumor, story, warning or personal request.

## 6. Faction activity
Unusual convoy, patrol, research operation or covert transfer.

## 7. Economic opportunity
Scarcity, unusual price spread, hidden supplier.

## 8. Lore discovery
Artifact, recording, registry fragment or historical evidence.

## 9. Mystery chain
A discovery that produces additional questions rather than immediate answers.

## 10. Emergent event
A world-state change that the player happens to encounter.
