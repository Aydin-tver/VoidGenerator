# Exploration Anomaly System v1.0

Anomalies are discoverable world objects or events.

An anomaly may expose:
- unusual telemetry;
- strange movement;
- unexplained signal;
- energy signature;
- altered cargo;
- damaged technology;
- inconsistent registry data.

Anomaly states:

UNSEEN
→ DETECTED
→ OBSERVED
→ INVESTIGATED
→ INTERPRETED
→ CORROBORATED
→ RESOLVED / MYSTERY

Equipment and skills determine what level of observation is possible.
