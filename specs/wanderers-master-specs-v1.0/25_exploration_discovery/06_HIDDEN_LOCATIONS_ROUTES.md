# Hidden Locations & Routes v1.0

Hidden locations should be discoverable through gameplay rather than universal map markers.

Sources:
- rumor;
- coordinates fragment;
- navigation anomaly;
- NPC knowledge;
- cargo data;
- old registry;
- sensor pattern.

Access can require:
- equipment;
- skill;
- faction permission;
- risk tolerance;
- special route knowledge.

A hidden route can provide:
- shorter travel;
- dangerous shortcut;
- smuggling opportunity;
- secret location;
- strategic advantage.

The route should become part of player knowledge after discovery.
