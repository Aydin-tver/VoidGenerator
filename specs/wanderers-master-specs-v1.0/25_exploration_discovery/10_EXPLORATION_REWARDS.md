# Exploration Rewards v1.0

Rewards should be varied.

Possible rewards:
- knowledge;
- unique equipment;
- technology;
- credits;
- cargo;
- route access;
- faction leverage;
- NPC relationship;
- lore;
- future mission;
- strategic advantage.

Knowledge can be the most valuable reward.

Exploration should sometimes produce no immediate material reward but significantly improve future decision quality.
