# Equipment × Faction × Economy v1.0

Equipment can have social and economic identity.

## Faction equipment

A faction may:
- sell restricted modules;
- provide prototype technology;
- require reputation;
- offer discounted maintenance;
- issue equipment tied to faction contracts.

## Economic effects

Equipment can create:
- new cargo markets;
- profitable routes;
- salvage opportunities;
- restricted goods opportunities;
- maintenance costs;
- energy costs;
- new services.

## Consequence example

Player acquires a rare faction prototype.

Possible effects:
- faction trust increases;
- rival faction becomes suspicious;
- black-market value appears;
- NPC researcher requests access;
- future mission changes.

The item therefore participates in the world rather than existing only in inventory.
