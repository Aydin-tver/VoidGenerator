# Equipment & Information Gameplay v1.0

Equipment can affect what the player knows.

## Information tiers

RAW
→ FILTERED
→ INTERPRETED
→ CORROBORATED
→ SPECIALIZED

Example:

Basic scanner:
> Unusual energy fluctuation.

Advanced scanner:
> Energy fluctuation follows a repeating sequence.

Signal decoder:
> Sequence contains structured transmission data.

Expert Sensors + decoder:
> Transmission resembles a known SIGMA pattern but includes an altered identifier.

The final interpretation remains subject to player knowledge and evidence.
