# Equipment & Exploration v1.0

Exploration should sometimes be capability-gated, not quest-marker-gated.

Examples:

Thermal Shielding:
→ enter a hazardous region.

Long-Range Scanner:
→ discover a weak signal before arrival.

Salvage Rig:
→ recover technology from a wreck.

Silent Drive:
→ approach a restricted site with reduced detection.

Signal Decoder:
→ understand a hidden transmission.

Cargo Isolation:
→ transport unstable material safely.

The player should discover some uses through knowledge, experimentation and rumors rather than explicit quest instructions.
