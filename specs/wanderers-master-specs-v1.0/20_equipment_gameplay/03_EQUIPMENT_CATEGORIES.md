# Equipment Categories v1.0

## Sensors
Scanners, signal analyzers, probes, tracking systems.

RPG impact:
- information;
- discovery;
- investigation;
- anomaly interpretation.

## Engineering
Repair systems, salvage rigs, diagnostic modules, reactor interfaces.

RPG impact:
- repairs;
- salvage;
- technical investigation;
- equipment interactions.

## Mobility
Engines, maneuvering systems, navigation modules, jump assistance.

RPG impact:
- route options;
- pursuit;
- escape;
- dangerous-region access.

## Cargo / Logistics
Cargo expansion, isolation, refrigeration, secure containers.

RPG impact:
- specialized trade;
- smuggling;
- faction logistics;
- economic opportunities.

## Covert
Signature suppression, silent drive, spoofing systems.

RPG impact:
- detection;
- attribution;
- restricted access;
- covert missions.

## Combat
Weapons and defensive systems.

RPG impact:
- combat approaches;
- intimidation;
- protection;
- target elimination.

Combat remains important, but equipment should also affect non-combat gameplay where logically appropriate.
