# Equipment & Narrative v1.0

Equipment can become part of the story.

## Narrative uses

- NPCs react to specialized technology.
- Factions recognize proprietary equipment.
- Equipment can provide evidence.
- Equipment can create new dialogue.
- Equipment can alter mission approaches.
- Equipment can reveal lore.
- Equipment can create obligations.

## Example

A player installs a prototype OmniCorp signal analyzer.

Nova recognizes the architecture.

This can create:
- technical dialogue;
- faction suspicion;
- a research request;
- evidence that the prototype existed;
- an alternative explanation for a previous anomaly.

The module becomes a narrative object.
