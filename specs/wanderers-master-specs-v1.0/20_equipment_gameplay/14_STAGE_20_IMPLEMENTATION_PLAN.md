# Stage 20 Implementation Plan

## Phase 1
Create:
- EquipmentCapability
- EquipmentAction
- EquipmentRequirement
- EquipmentRisk
- EquipmentInteraction

## Phase 2
Connect capabilities to authoritative systems:
- scanner
- flight
- cargo
- repair
- combat
- trade
- station services

## Phase 3
Connect equipment to narrative:
- missions
- knowledge
- dialogue
- factions
- exploration
- economy

## Phase 4
Rework the Ferrum vertical slice around capability-based approaches.

## Phase 5
QA:
- actions per item
- non-combat uses
- unique information
- mission approaches
- skill synergies
- faction/economic interactions

## Acceptance gate

Stage 20 passes when:
- 10+ equipment capabilities are defined;
- major equipment has at least one non-combat use;
- equipment changes mission approaches;
- equipment can change information quality;
- equipment has meaningful risks/trade-offs;
- at least four builds use different equipment packages;
- equipment does not become a set of binary quest keys.
