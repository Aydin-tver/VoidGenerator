# Equipment Risk / Reward v1.0

Powerful equipment should create choices.

Examples:

### Long-range scanner
Benefit:
- earlier information.

Trade-off:
- higher energy consumption;
- possible signal visibility.

### Silent drive
Benefit:
- reduced detection.

Trade-off:
- lower acceleration;
- higher heat.

### Salvage rig
Benefit:
- access to valuable wrecks.

Trade-off:
- slower escape;
- more exposure during salvage.

### Deep scanner
Benefit:
- rare evidence.

Trade-off:
- longer scan time;
- increased anomaly attention.

The strongest module is not necessarily the universally best module.
