# Equipment Upgrade Rules v1.0

## Upgrade dimensions

Do not rely only on numeric Mk levels.

An upgrade may improve:

- range;
- precision;
- energy efficiency;
- speed;
- reliability;
- information resolution;
- risk reduction;
- action duration;
- capability combinations.

## Example

Scanner Mk1:
detect anomaly.

Scanner Mk2:
classify anomaly.

Scanner Mk3:
compare anomaly against known patterns.

Scanner Mk4:
detect manipulated signal components.

Scanner Mk5:
reconstruct fragments under appropriate skill conditions.

Thus upgrades expand information and agency, not only numbers.
