# Main Story × Exploration v1.0

Major story truths should have optional exploration routes.

Examples:
- abandoned relay;
- lost research vessel;
- hidden archive;
- secret route;
- corrupted station module;
- forgotten Voidari site.

Exploration can:
- reveal optional evidence;
- bypass a faction gate;
- strengthen a theory;
- contradict an NPC;
- unlock alternative dialogue;
- change a later choice.

The main story should remain completable without finding every secret.
