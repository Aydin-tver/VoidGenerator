# Main Story × Economy v1.0

The central story should affect and be affected by the economy.

Examples:

Research project creates component demand.

Demand creates unusual procurement patterns.

Player investigates the market.

Faction competition increases prices.

Security responds to strategic cargo.

NPCs gain or lose opportunities.

The player can use:
- money;
- supply;
- contracts;
- routes;
- market information;
- scarcity.

Economic play can reveal story information without being a forced quest.
