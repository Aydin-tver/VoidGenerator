# Campaign Structure v1.0

The campaign retains the 8-act architecture but each act becomes a systemic problem space.

## A0 — Ordinary Work
Purpose:
establish ship, economy, NPC relationships and player autonomy.

Systems:
- commerce;
- piloting;
- equipment;
- station NPCs.

Player experience:
The world should already be playable before the mystery begins.

## A1 — Anomalies
The player notices inconsistencies in routine operations.

Systems:
- sensors;
- exploration;
- evidence;
- NPC dialogue.

## A2 — Contradictions
Different sources disagree.

Systems:
- information gameplay;
- social;
- investigation;
- faction access.

## A3 — Faction Conflict
Factions interpret the same evidence differently.

Systems:
- faction relationships;
- economy;
- missions;
- negotiation;
- espionage/covert play.

## A4 — Algorithmic Conflict
The player discovers that some events are not isolated.

Systems:
- world simulation;
- NPC networks;
- hidden routes;
- economic patterns.

## A5 — SIGMA
Evidence points toward an older intelligence architecture.

Systems:
- specialized equipment;
- exploration;
- technical skills;
- information trading.

## A6 — Voidari / Zazor
The player uncovers the consciousness-transfer history.

Systems:
- faction secrets;
- lore;
- NPC trust;
- competing interpretations.

## A7 — Thread / Identity / Choice
The player must decide what kind of intelligence they are becoming.

Systems:
- identity;
- relationships;
- factions;
- information control;
- world consequences.

The acts should overlap rather than behave like hard chapter walls.
