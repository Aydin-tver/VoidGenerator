# Main Story Replayability v1.0

Replayability comes from state variation, not random dialogue.

Different playthroughs should differ through:

- faction alignment;
- NPC relationships;
- discovered evidence;
- equipment;
- skills;
- economic choices;
- exploration;
- secrets revealed;
- failed missions;
- player identity.

Target:
A player completing the campaign twice should encounter materially different context in at least 30% of major story situations.
