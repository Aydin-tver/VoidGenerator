# Main Story × Factions v1.0

Each faction should interpret the central mystery differently.

## OmniCorp
Sees technology as strategic and commercial leverage.

## Free Merchants
Focus on autonomy, survival and freedom of information.

## Tidari
Prioritize registry, continuity and institutional stability.

## Arcani
Treat the phenomenon as a threat to established knowledge.

## Khadar
Approach it through security, survival or strategic control.

## Voidari
Connected to the origin of the dangerous technology.

## Choir
Represents an alternative interpretation of identity/consciousness.

The player should be able to:
- support;
- oppose;
- deceive;
- trade information;
- remain independent;
- exploit conflicts;
- broker cooperation.

No faction should have the complete truth.
