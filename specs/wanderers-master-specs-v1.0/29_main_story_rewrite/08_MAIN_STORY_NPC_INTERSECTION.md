# Main Story × NPCs v1.0

Major NPCs should carry fragments of the campaign.

Examples:

Lena Voss:
- practical evidence from convoy routes;
- personal concern about autonomous systems;
- relationship-dependent information.

Dispatcher Tidari:
- registry anomalies;
- institutional perspective;
- archival contradictions.

Engineer Nova:
- technical interpretation;
- equipment evidence;
- engineering hypotheses.

Other recurring NPCs should represent:
- faction interpretations;
- economic interests;
- personal consequences;
- historical memory;
- conflicting beliefs.

NPCs can disagree with the player's interpretation.
