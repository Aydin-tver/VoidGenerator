# Main Story Endings v1.0

The five existing ending directions remain:

## Containment
The dangerous technology is restricted.

## Disclosure
Information is released despite instability.

## Independence
The player chooses autonomous existence outside faction control.

## Non-intervention
The player refuses to determine the fate of the technology.

## Open Continuation
The player preserves an unresolved path into the future.

Endings should be determined by accumulated:
- choices;
- faction states;
- relationships;
- knowledge;
- player identity;
- world consequences.

Do not use a single morality score.
