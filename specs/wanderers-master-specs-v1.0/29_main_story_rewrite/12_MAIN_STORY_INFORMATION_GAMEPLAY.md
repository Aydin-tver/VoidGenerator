# Main Story Information Gameplay v1.0

The player should not automatically receive canonical conclusions.

For major mysteries:

```text
OBSERVATION
→ FRAGMENT
→ INTERPRETATION
→ HYPOTHESIS
→ CORROBORATION
→ THEORY
→ CHOICE
```

Different players may hold different theories.

NPCs react to:
- what the player knows;
- what they believe;
- what evidence they possess;
- what they reveal.

Critical revelations should have multiple supporting paths.

Contradictions should remain meaningful.
