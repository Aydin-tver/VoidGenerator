# Main Story × Skills & Equipment v1.0

Capabilities create alternative routes.

### Sensors
Find evidence.

### Engineering
Interpret/recover technology.

### Commerce
Discover economic patterns and negotiate procurement.

### Social
Gain testimony and cooperation.

### Covert
Investigate restricted operations.

### Piloting
Reach dangerous/hidden locations.

Equipment can create capabilities unavailable to another build.

A player should occasionally think:

> "I solved this because of how I built my ship."

That is a core RPG outcome.
