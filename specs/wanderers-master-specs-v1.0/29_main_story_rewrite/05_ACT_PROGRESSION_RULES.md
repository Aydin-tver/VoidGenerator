# Act Progression Rules v1.0

An act advances through knowledge/state thresholds, not only mission completion.

Each act has:

- required knowledge;
- possible evidence;
- world-state conditions;
- faction states;
- NPC relationships;
- capability routes;
- optional discoveries.

Progression states:

LOCKED
→ AVAILABLE
→ INVESTIGATING
→ UNDERSTOOD
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ TRANSITION

A player may enter an act with different evidence sets.

The story adapts its presentation to what the player already knows.
