# Player Identity Arc v1.0

The player begins with limited understanding of their own continuity.

Identity progression:

UNKNOWN
→ FUNCTIONAL
→ SELF-AWARE
→ CURIOUS
→ SELF-DEFINING
→ CONFLICTED
→ AUTONOMOUS
→ RESPONSIBLE

Important:

The game must not tell the player what identity they have.

The player constructs it through choices.

Possible identity tendencies:
- loyal;
- independent;
- utilitarian;
- protective;
- curious;
- secretive;
- truth-seeking;
- pragmatic;
- faction-aligned.

These are tendencies, not morality meters.

Identity should emerge from remembered actions and consequences.
