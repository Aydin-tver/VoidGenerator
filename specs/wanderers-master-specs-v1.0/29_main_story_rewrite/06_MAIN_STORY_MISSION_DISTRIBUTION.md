# Main Story Mission Distribution v1.0

Target: 30–40 major missions.

Suggested distribution:

A0: 3–4
A1: 4–5
A2: 4–5
A3: 4–6
A4: 4–5
A5: 4–5
A6: 4–5
A7: 3–5

Not every mission is mandatory.

Some act progress can come from:
- exploration;
- faction missions;
- NPC investigations;
- economic actions;
- evidence aggregation.

At least 70% of major missions should provide 3+ meaningful approaches.

At least 20% should allow emergent/systemic solutions.
