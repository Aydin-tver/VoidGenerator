# Mission Density v1.0

Mission count alone is insufficient.

Each major mission should record:

- problem;
- player goal;
- available approaches;
- required capabilities;
- information;
- NPC involvement;
- faction involvement;
- economic context;
- exploration possibilities;
- failure state;
- partial success;
- full success;
- exceptional success;
- immediate consequence;
- medium consequence;
- long consequence;
- future hooks.

## Diversity target

Across major content, maintain a healthy distribution of:

- investigation;
- negotiation;
- trade;
- escort;
- exploration;
- recovery;
- sabotage;
- information brokerage;
- faction conflict;
- combat;
- survival;
- smuggling;
- discovery.

No single mission type should dominate the campaign.
