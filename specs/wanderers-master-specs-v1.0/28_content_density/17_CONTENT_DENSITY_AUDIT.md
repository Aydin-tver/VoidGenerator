# Content Density Audit v1.0

For each release calculate:

## Quantity
- missions;
- NPCs;
- stations;
- discoveries;
- evidence;
- equipment capabilities;
- faction conflicts;
- dynamic events.

## Quality
- average tier;
- systemic interactions;
- approach count;
- consequence count;
- replay variation.

## Coverage
- skills;
- equipment;
- factions;
- economy;
- exploration;
- dialogue;
- world simulation.

## Risk indicators

RED:
- repeated quest templates;
- systems without content;
- factions with reputation-only gameplay;
- NPCs with no memory;
- exploration with no consequences;
- economy with one optimal route;
- equipment with only stat bonuses.

YELLOW:
- low variation;
- weak consequences;
- underused skills;
- shallow NPC arcs.

GREEN:
- multiple approaches;
- systemic reuse;
- persistent consequences;
- independent player objectives.
