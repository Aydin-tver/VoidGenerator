# Player Path Density v1.0

Major situations should support multiple approaches.

Target:

- 70%+ of major missions: 3+ meaningful approaches.
- 40%+ of major missions: 4+ approaches.
- 20%+ of major missions: systemic/emergent solutions.

Approaches may include:
- capability;
- equipment;
- faction access;
- information;
- negotiation;
- economic leverage;
- exploration;
- combat;
- avoidance;
- deception.

Failure should usually create a new state rather than erase progress.
