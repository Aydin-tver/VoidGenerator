# NPC Density v1.0

A recurring NPC is not simply a portrait and dialogue node.

Deep NPC requirements:

- independent goal;
- needs/fears;
- faction/internal-group relationship;
- capabilities;
- secrets;
- memory;
- relationship state;
- at least one personal conflict;
- at least one player-dependent future state.

## Distribution

30–40 recurring NPCs:
- 10–12 faction actors;
- 8–10 station professionals;
- 5–8 merchants/operators;
- 5–8 researchers/technical figures;
- 3–5 antagonistic or ambiguous actors.

At least 15 should have deep personal arcs.
