# Handcrafted vs Systemic Content v1.0

Not everything should be handcrafted.

## Handcrafted

Use for:
- main story;
- major faction arcs;
- major NPC arcs;
- signature mysteries;
- important lore;
- unique locations.

## Systemic

Use for:
- trade opportunities;
- shortages;
- security responses;
- NPC requests;
- route incidents;
- dynamic missions;
- market events.

## Hybrid

Best for:
- faction conflicts;
- exploration chains;
- economic mysteries;
- recurring NPC problems.

Systemic generation must obey authored world rules.
