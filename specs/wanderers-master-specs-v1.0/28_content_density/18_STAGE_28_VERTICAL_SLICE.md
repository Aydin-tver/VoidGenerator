# Stage 28 Vertical Slice — Content Density Test

Use the Ferrum region as a content-density laboratory.

Minimum local slice:

### Stations
3 meaningful stations.

### NPCs
8 recurring NPCs.

### Factions
4 active factions.

### Missions
12 missions:
- 3 main;
- 3 faction;
- 2 NPC;
- 2 exploration;
- 2 dynamic.

### Discoveries
10.

### Mystery chains
3.

### Economic situations
5.

### Equipment capabilities
15.

### Evidence/lore
25.

### World events
10.

### Approaches
At least 3 for most major missions.

The slice passes only if these assets interact rather than existing as separate lists.
