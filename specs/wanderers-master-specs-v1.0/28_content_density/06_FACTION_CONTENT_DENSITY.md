# Faction Content Density v1.0

Each major faction should provide:

- public identity;
- internal groups;
- strategic goal;
- internal conflict;
- economic interest;
- technology access;
- secrets;
- relationship with at least 3 other factions;
- player progression;
- faction-specific missions;
- consequences of player support/opposition.

## Minimum

7 major factions.

Each:
- 6–10 meaningful faction missions;
- 2–3 internal tensions;
- 3+ recurring NPC representatives;
- 2+ unique systemic rewards/access paths;
- 2+ major secrets or disputed truths.

A faction that only changes reputation numbers is incomplete.
