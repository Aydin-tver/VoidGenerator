# Content Quality Tiers v1.0

## Tier 0 — Decoration

Pure flavor.

Examples:
- background text;
- cosmetic naming;
- repeated generic dialogue.

Does not count toward meaningful density.

## Tier 1 — Functional

Provides a useful action or reward.

Example:
- basic delivery;
- simple purchase.

Limited systemic value.

## Tier 2 — Choice

Offers a meaningful decision.

Example:
- choose supplier;
- decide whom to trust.

## Tier 3 — Systemic

Interacts with multiple RPG systems.

Example:
- faction + economy + NPC + equipment.

## Tier 4 — Emergent

Produces consequences that affect future opportunities.

Example:
- player manipulates a shortage, changing a faction's strategy.

## Tier 5 — Signature

Unique content strongly associated with Wanderers.

Example:
- Information Gameplay mystery involving contradictory evidence, factions and player identity.

Target:
Major content should average Tier 3+.
Signature content should exist in every major arc.
