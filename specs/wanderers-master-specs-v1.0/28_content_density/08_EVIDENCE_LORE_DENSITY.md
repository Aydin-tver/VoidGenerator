# Evidence & Lore Density v1.0

Lore should be distributed across gameplay.

Target:
- 150+ meaningful evidence/lore objects.

Sources:
- NPC testimony;
- logs;
- registry;
- cargo;
- scans;
- artifacts;
- missions;
- economic records;
- faction intelligence;
- exploration.

Critical truths should normally have multiple independent paths.

Avoid lore dumps.

The player should construct understanding from fragments.
