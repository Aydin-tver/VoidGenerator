# Content Pacing v1.0

Avoid long stretches of one content type.

Recommended rhythm:

DISCOVERY
→ ACTION
→ SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE
→ EXPLORATION
→ ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY
→ CONFLICT
→ CHARACTER MOMENT
→ MYSTERY
→ FACTION DECISION

The player should regularly encounter:
- something to do;
- something to learn;
- something to decide;
- something that changes.

Mobile pacing should support sessions of:
- 3–5 minutes;
- 10–15 minutes;
- 20–30 minutes.

A session should be capable of producing a meaningful state change.
