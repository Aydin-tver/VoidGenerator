# Content Targets v1.0

These are production targets, not promises that every item must ship simultaneously.

## Campaign

- Main story: 30–40 major missions
- Main story dialogue/scenes: 80–120
- Main story evidence/lore objects: 60+

## Factions

- 7 major factions
- 2–3 meaningful internal groups per faction
- 8–15 faction conflicts
- 50–70 faction missions
- 20+ faction-specific rewards/access paths

## Characters

- 30–40 recurring named NPCs
- 15+ NPCs with deep personal arcs
- 100+ persistent relationship-changing interactions
- 50+ NPC-generated opportunities/missions

## Exploration

- 30–50 exploration discoveries
- 15–25 mystery chains
- 100+ secrets/evidence fragments
- 10–20 hidden locations
- 10–20 hidden routes/opportunities

## Economy

- 30–50 economically meaningful goods
- 20+ meaningful economic events
- 15+ strategic shortages/surpluses
- 10+ high-value trade opportunities
- 3+ viable economic playstyles

## Equipment

- 50+ meaningful equipment items/modules
- 50+ unique capabilities
- 15+ non-combat equipment interactions
- 10+ build-defining equipment combinations

## Dynamic world

- 30+ dynamic mission seeds
- 20+ world events
- 20+ persistent world-state transitions
- 10+ systemic conflict patterns

## Stations / regions

- 20–30 meaningful stations/locations
- 8–12 major regions/systems
- each major station should expose multiple systems:
  economy + NPC + faction + mission + discovery.
