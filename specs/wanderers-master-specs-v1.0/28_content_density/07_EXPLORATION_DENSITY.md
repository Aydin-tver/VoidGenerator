# Exploration Density v1.0

Exploration should be distributed throughout the game.

Target:

- 30–50 standalone discoveries;
- 15–25 mystery chains;
- 100+ evidence/lore fragments;
- 10–20 hidden locations;
- 10–20 hidden routes;
- 20+ unusual signals/observations.

Discovery categories should include:
- abandoned technology;
- lost ships;
- hidden operations;
- anomalies;
- historical evidence;
- economic opportunities;
- faction secrets;
- Thread/Voidari clues;
- environmental mysteries.

At least some discoveries should remain unresolved.
