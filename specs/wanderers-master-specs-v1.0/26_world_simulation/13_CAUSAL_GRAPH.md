# World Causal Graph v1.0

The world should support chains such as:

```text
PLAYER SELLS RARE ORE
        ↓
STATION STOCK INCREASES
        ↓
PRICE FALLS
        ↓
MERCHANT ROUTE CHANGES
        ↓
PIRATE INTEREST MOVES
        ↓
SECURITY SHIFTS
        ↓
FACTION RESPONSE
        ↓
NPC GOALS CHANGE
        ↓
NEW MISSION / DISCOVERY
```

This is the central systemic RPG property.

A player's economic action can eventually become a narrative consequence.

The same applies to:
- combat;
- exploration;
- faction choices;
- information;
- dialogue;
- equipment;
- mission failure.
