# Stage 26 Vertical Slice — Ferrum Trade Corridor

## Initial state

Ferrum has:
- moderate ore demand;
- normal security;
- Free Merchant traffic;
- OmniCorp interest;
- small pirate presence.

## Player action

Player discovers a rare ore source and sells a large amount at Ferrum.

## Simulation

1. station inventory rises;
2. local price falls;
3. merchant demand shifts;
4. route traffic changes;
5. pirates notice a more valuable alternative route;
6. security reallocates patrols;
7. OmniCorp evaluates supply opportunity;
8. Free Merchants react;
9. NPC goals change;
10. new missions/discoveries become possible.

## Alternative

Player withholds the ore.

Result:
- shortage persists;
- price remains high;
- piracy pressure stays high;
- security increases;
- merchant missions appear;
- faction competition intensifies.

Same world, different player impact.

## Acceptance

The player must be able to observe at least some downstream consequences.
