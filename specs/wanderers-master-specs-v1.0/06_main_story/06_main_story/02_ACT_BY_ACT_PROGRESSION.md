# Wanderers — Act-by-Act Progression v0.5

## A0 — Arrival

### Purpose
Establish the player's ordinary role, ship, stations and first relationships.

### Player knows
- local travel;
- station economy;
- basic factions;
- basic ship limitations.

### Player does
- trade / travel / basic mission;
- first contact with Lena or local station characters;
- first practical decision.

### Hidden layer
A small anomaly is introduced but not explained.

### Exit condition
Player has enough ordinary-world context to recognize that later anomalies are abnormal.

---

## A1 — Anomalies

### Central question
Why are routes, registries or convoy information inconsistent?

### Required playable beats
1. convoy problem;
2. route anomaly;
3. registry discrepancy;
4. first evidence item;
5. first faction request.

### Key relationship
Lena ↔ player.

### Branch
Disclose / withhold information.

### Important rule
The choice changes trust/access/evidence availability; it does not immediately split the entire campaign.

---

## A2 — Contradictions

### Central question
Is there a single mundane explanation?

### Required beats
- independent corroboration;
- Ferrum investigation;
- technical interpretation;
- conflicting faction explanations;
- evidence contradiction.

### Player learns
The anomaly is not safely explained as one local incident.

### Convergence
Different routes may produce different evidence, but all major routes eventually establish the larger mystery.

---

## A3 — Algorithm War

### Central question
What historical conflict shaped today's AI/institutional order?

### Actors
KarmaCore, Mitra, SIGMA, Registry and relevant factions.

### Player involvement
The player does not receive a history lecture. Instead:
- finds records;
- compares conflicting accounts;
- encounters consequences of old decisions;
- receives incompatible interpretations.

### Reveal
The historical algorithm conflict is causally connected to current anomalies.

### Branch
Player may trust one interpretation, another, or remain uncertain.

---

## A4 — The Gap

### Central question
What exists outside the official model of identity and authority?

### Narrative functions
- introduce Zazor as a lived alternative, not a lore dump;
- introduce Voidari as an identity/ownership question;
- expose limits of official records;
- connect personal identity to broader technology.

### Hard rule
Do not fully explain Consciousness Transfer here.

### Player outcome
A new hypothesis becomes plausible:
identity may not be inseparable from one physical substrate.

---

## A5 — The Thread

### Central question
What is the Thread and why does it react?

### Gameplay anchors
Thread resonance, discovery, evidence, exploration and encounters where supported.

### Revelation structure
1. resonance;
2. repeatable pattern;
3. independent corroboration;
4. competing interpretations;
5. SIGMA/Voidari/Choir perspectives.

### Player learns
The Thread is not merely an old infrastructure component.

### Unresolved
Its ultimate origin and purpose remain partially unknown.

---

## A6 — Choice

### Central question
What should the player do with incomplete knowledge?

Possible narrative directions:
- controlled disclosure;
- institutional containment;
- independent investigation;
- preservation/non-intervention;
- cooperation with selected actors.

These are story directions, not morality labels.

### Rule
No branch should be named "good" or "evil".

### Consequence preparation
A6 records durable state that A7 can interpret.

---

## A7 — Consequence

### Purpose
Make prior decisions visible.

Ending sequence is assembled from:
- player identity state;
- faction relationships;
- evidence/knowledge;
- character states;
- world consequences;
- Thread interpretation;
- unresolved mysteries.

### Rule
The final act must answer:
"What changed because of what the player actually did?"
