# Wanderers — Main Story Master Specification v0.5

## Purpose

This document turns the existing eight-act narrative architecture into a production skeleton.

It does not replace the canon. It defines:
- player knowledge progression;
- causal mission progression;
- faction/character participation;
- evidence/revelation gates;
- branch points;
- convergence rules;
- final-state preparation.

## Core narrative axis

```text
ORDINARY WORK
    ↓
ANOMALIES
    ↓
CONTRADICTIONS
    ↓
FACTION CONFLICT
    ↓
ALGORITHMIC CONFLICT
    ↓
SIGMA
    ↓
ZAZOR / VOIDARI
    ↓
THREAD
    ↓
PLAYER IDENTITY
    ↓
CHOICE
    ↓
CONSEQUENCE
```

## Act overview

| Act | Name | Central question | Player state after act |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | Arrival | What is this ship/system? | player understands ordinary work |
| A1 | Anomalies | Why are ordinary systems behaving strangely? | first evidence |
| A2 | Contradictions | Is this human error, fraud or something deeper? | competing explanations |
| A3 | Algorithm War | What historical conflict produced the present system? | algorithmic actors become relevant |
| A4 | The Gap | What does Zazor/Voidari reveal about identity and control? | official explanations lose exclusivity |
| A5 | The Thread | What is actually responding to intelligent systems? | Thread becomes active mystery |
| A6 | Choice | Who gets to interpret/control/respond to this discovery? | player commits to a direction |
| A7 | Consequence | What did the accumulated decisions actually create? | ending state |

## Non-negotiable structure

Every act must contain:
1. playable problem;
2. at least one investigation/discovery;
3. at least one NPC/faction interaction;
4. at least one state transition;
5. a follow-up hook.

No act may be completed only by reading dialogue.

## Narrative pacing

The player should repeatedly cycle:

```text
work → anomaly → decision → action → evidence → consequence → new question
```

The main story is therefore a chain of causal discoveries, not a sequence of exposition scenes.
