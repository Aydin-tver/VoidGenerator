# Exploration & Discovery Requirements v1.0

Exploration must not depend entirely on quest markers.

Discovery sources:
- rumors
- partial coordinates
- registry fragments
- NPC testimony
- anomalous signals
- old maps
- cargo manifests
- faction intelligence
- environmental clues

Discovery targets:
- hidden routes
- wrecks
- abandoned stations
- secret relays
- pirate bases
- anomalies
- lost technology
- Voidari traces
- Thread phenomena
- hidden markets

## Discovery quality

A discovery should ideally provide at least one:
- new knowledge
- new location
- new capability
- new mission
- new faction opportunity
- new economic opportunity
- new lore interpretation
