# Faction System Requirements v1.0

Each major faction must eventually define:

- public ideology
- actual goals
- fears
- enemies
- allies
- internal groups
- ranks
- rank requirements
- privileges
- faction technologies
- faction services
- faction economy
- faction secrets
- recurring mission types
- internal conflicts
- reactions to player actions
- relationship to other factions

## Faction gameplay

Faction progression must unlock gameplay, not only reputation numbers.

Possible privileges:
- routes
- contracts
- technology
- discounts
- intelligence
- protection
- restricted markets
- unique missions
- special equipment
- safe passage

## Internal conflict

Every major faction should contain at least two interests that can disagree.

This prevents a faction from becoming a single NPC with a reputation bar.
