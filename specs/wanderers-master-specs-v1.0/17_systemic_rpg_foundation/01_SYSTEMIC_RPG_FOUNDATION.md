# Wanderers of the Void — Systemic RPG Foundation v1.0

## Purpose

Stage 17 establishes the common gameplay foundation for the next RPG systems:

- Player Agency
- Skills & Specializations
- Equipment-as-Gameplay
- Faction Simulation
- NPC Simulation
- Dialogue Gameplay
- Systemic Quests
- Exploration & Discovery
- World Simulation
- Economic RPG

The goal is not to add more lore. The goal is to make the existing lore, missions and consequences operate as one systemic RPG.

## Core principle

A major player action must be able to change more than one isolated number.

Preferred chain:

PLAYER INTENT
→ INFORMATION / PREPARATION
→ ACTION
→ IMMEDIATE RESULT
→ NPC / FACTION / ECONOMY / WORLD REACTION
→ NEW INFORMATION
→ NEW OPPORTUNITIES

A reward-only result is insufficient for a major RPG action.

## Design pillars

1. Player forms goals instead of only receiving goals.
2. Important objectives have multiple viable approaches.
3. Capabilities change what the player can do.
4. Knowledge is a gameplay resource.
5. NPCs remember meaningful interactions.
6. Factions have internal interests, ranks and conflicts.
7. Consequences propagate through the world.
8. Exploration can create goals without quest markers.
9. Economy can create and solve problems.
10. Main story emerges from systems instead of bypassing them.

## Non-goals

This stage does not require:
- autonomous walking NPC simulation;
- a full political simulator;
- unrestricted player dialogue authoring at runtime;
- procedural quest generation;
- replacing authoritative combat/trade/flight state.

Narrative systems must remain adapters over authoritative game systems.
