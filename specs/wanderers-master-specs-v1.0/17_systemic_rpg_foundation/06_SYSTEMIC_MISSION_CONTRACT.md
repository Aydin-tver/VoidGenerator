# Systemic Mission Contract v1.0

Every major mission must define:

- premise
- player-visible information
- hidden information
- possible player goals
- approaches
- required capabilities
- optional capabilities
- NPC approaches
- faction approaches
- economic approaches
- combat approach
- stealth approach
- investigation approach
- failure state
- partial success
- major success
- immediate consequences
- delayed consequences
- world consequences
- NPC consequences
- faction consequences
- future content consequences

## Required QA

A major mission fails review if:
- only one practical solution exists without a deliberate reason;
- failure is equivalent to success except for reward;
- choices do not alter future state;
- capability gates only lock content instead of changing approach;
- mission can be completed without interacting with its intended RPG system.
