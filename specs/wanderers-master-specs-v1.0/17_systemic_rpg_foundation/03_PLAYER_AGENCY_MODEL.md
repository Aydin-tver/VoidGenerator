# Player Agency Model v1.0

## Agency definition

Player agency is the ability to:
1. form or adopt a goal,
2. choose an approach,
3. use available capabilities,
4. accept risk,
5. produce consequences,
6. pursue a different goal afterwards.

## Agency dimensions

### Goal Agency
Player can create a meaningful objective from information, rumor, discovery or personal interest.

### Approach Agency
The same objective can be approached through different gameplay methods.

### Information Agency
Player chooses who receives, receives not, sells, verifies or hides information.

### Faction Agency
Player can cooperate with multiple groups without every interaction being a binary membership choice.

### Economic Agency
Player can exploit, support, disrupt or avoid economic opportunities.

### Identity Agency
Repeated behavior changes the player's emerging AI identity and how others interpret it.

## Agency Score

0 — one fixed path.
1 — cosmetic dialogue choice.
2 — two outcomes.
3 — multiple outcomes.
4 — multiple gameplay approaches.
5 — player can independently form and pursue the objective.

Target:
- ordinary mission: >= 3
- major mission: >= 4
- major mystery/faction arc: >= 5

## Anti-railroading rule

A branch is not meaningful if all branches:
- give the same immediate result,
- produce the same world state,
- unlock the same future content,
- and differ only in dialogue text.

At least one of these should differ for a consequential choice:
- access
- relationship
- information
- economy
- faction state
- world state
- future mission
- equipment opportunity
