# Economic RPG Requirements v1.0

Economy should respond to:

- supply
- demand
- scarcity
- distance
- route security
- faction control
- piracy
- conflict
- player actions
- major world events

## Player economic agency

Player should eventually be able to:
- discover profitable routes
- create opportunities
- exploit scarcity
- transport legal goods
- transport restricted goods
- support faction logistics
- disrupt markets
- profit from information

## Economy-story interaction

Economic actions may:
- change faction influence
- change station prosperity
- create missions
- remove missions
- alter NPC behavior
- reveal information
- create shortages
