# Content Density & QA Targets v1.0

## Long-term content targets

- 30–40 recurring named NPCs
- 20–30 meaningful stations/locations
- 80–120 meaningful missions
- 25–30 systemic major missions
- 100+ discoveries/secrets
- 150+ evidence/lore items
- 20+ faction conflicts
- 30–50 world-state transitions
- 50+ equipment/capability interactions

These are targets, not a requirement to ship all content at once.

## Gate

Content expansion must not proceed at scale until the systemic vertical slice demonstrates:
- agency >= 4
- systemic density >= 5
- at least 3 viable approaches
- at least 2 meaningful outcomes
- at least one delayed consequence
- at least one world reaction
- at least one new opportunity generated by the player's action.
