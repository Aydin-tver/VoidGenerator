# Build Identity v1.0

A build emerges from:
Skills + Specialization + Equipment + Knowledge + Faction privileges + Player behavior.

### Signal Investigator
Sensors 5 / Engineering 3 / Social 3.
Strong in mysteries, anomaly research and evidence.

### Independent Broker
Commerce 5 / Social 4 / Sensors 2.
Strong in information markets and negotiation.

### Salvage Specialist
Engineering 5 / Sensors 4 / Piloting 3.
Strong in wrecks, abandoned technology and dangerous exploration.

### Covert Courier
Piloting 4 / Covert 5 / Commerce 3.
Strong in restricted routes, smuggling and evasion.

Builds should expose different content and risks rather than create a simple power ranking.
