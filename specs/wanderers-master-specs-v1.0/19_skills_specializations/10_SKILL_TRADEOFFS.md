# Skill Trade-offs v1.0

Meaningful specialization requires opportunity cost.

Trade-offs:
- access vs access
- information vs speed
- safety vs profit
- faction trust vs independence
- certainty vs risk
- technical vs social capability

Example: Sensors can verify independently; Social can obtain information faster through people; Commerce can purchase information.

Expertise should provide different methods, more information, safer options or unique opportunities—not automatic dominance.
