# Skill × Equipment Interaction v1.0

Equipment amplifies skills; skills give equipment new uses.

Examples:
- Advanced scanner + Sensors 4 → reconstruct historical signal fragments.
- Advanced scanner + Sensors 1 → raw readings.
- Engineering 4 + damaged reactor → diagnose hidden instability.
- Engineering 1 + same reactor → basic repair or pay an NPC.
- Commerce 4 + cargo scanner → identify unusual demand and profitable cargo.

Do not make every item a binary skill key. Equipment can change information quality, risk, available actions and opportunities.
