# Skill × NPC/Faction Interaction v1.0

Skills affect how characters and factions can be approached.

Engineering → technical missions, specialist evidence, advanced repair services.
Commerce → trade intelligence, contracts, restricted markets.
Sensors → anomaly research and signal-analysis missions.
Social → negotiation, confidential information, mediation.

High skill does not simply mean higher friendship. It means the NPC/faction has a reason to offer a different interaction.
