# Wanderers of the Void — Skills & Specializations v1.0

## Goal
Skills are a source of player agency. A skill must change what the player can attempt, not merely provide a percentage bonus.

Weak: `Engineering +10% repair speed`.
Strong: `Engineering 4 → diagnose an unknown reactor fault and choose a safer repair`.

## Skill roles
- new actions
- alternative approaches
- additional information
- reduced risk
- unique dialogue
- exploration methods
- equipment interactions
- faction access
- economic opportunities
- improved failure outcomes

Every major specialization should provide passive benefits, active actions, information advantages, at least one unique solution path, equipment/NPC/faction interactions, and trade-offs.
