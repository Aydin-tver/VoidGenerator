# Skill-Based Mission Design v1.0

Objective: determine whether the Ferrum anomaly is natural or artificial.

Sensors → scan and compare signal patterns.
Engineering → analyze energy signatures and hardware damage.
Social → question workers and identify conflicting testimony.
Commerce → trace unusual shipments.
Covert → investigate restricted areas without immediate attribution.
Piloting → approach through a dangerous route and observe directly.

Combinations create stronger approaches:
Sensors + Engineering → technical interpretation.
Social + Commerce → institutional/economic interpretation.
Covert + Sensors → independent evidence with lower attribution.

The same mystery should support different builds without procedural generation.
