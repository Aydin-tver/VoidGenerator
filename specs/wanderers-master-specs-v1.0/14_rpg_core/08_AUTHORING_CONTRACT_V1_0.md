# 08 — RPG Authoring Contract v1.0

Every major mission/scene should declare:

```text
narrative_purpose
player_goal
known_information
hidden_information
required_capabilities
alternative_capabilities
npc_context
faction_context
possible_choices
immediate_consequences
delayed_consequences
world_reaction
new_information
follow_up
```

## Mandatory questions

1. What does the player know?
2. What do they not know?
3. Why can they choose?
4. What gameplay expresses the choice?
5. Who remembers?
6. What changes?
7. What can be discovered later?
8. Can the player continue after failure?
9. Can another build solve it differently?
10. Is the content still interesting without the main quest marker?

If the answers are mostly "no", the content is not ready for Morrowind-level
production.
