# Risk & Commitment Model v1.0

Agency requires meaningful risk.

## Risk dimensions

- credits
- cargo
- ship damage
- reputation
- faction trust
- NPC relationship
- time
- future access
- information exposure
- world instability

## Commitment

Before a major action, the player should sometimes choose:

SAFE
BALANCED
RISKY

But these are not difficulty buttons.

Each commitment must change the actual action model.

Example:

SAFE:
- pay for official verification
- slower
- low reputation risk

RISKY:
- investigate independently
- faster
- higher detection risk
- potentially unique evidence

## Rule

Risk must be legible enough to support an informed choice, but hidden consequences may remain undisclosed.
