# Agency Score QA v1.0

## Score

### 0 — Rail
One path and one meaningful outcome.

### 1 — Cosmetic
Dialogue or presentation changes only.

### 2 — Branch
Two authored outcomes.

### 3 — Multi-solution
Three or more practical approaches.

### 4 — Systemic
Approaches use different gameplay systems and produce different consequences.

### 5 — Emergent
Player can formulate the objective from information and pursue it using multiple systems without a prescribed solution.

## Review questions

1. Could the player reasonably decide to do something other than the quest's suggested action?
2. Can the player achieve the underlying goal in another way?
3. Can the player fail without simply losing all progress?
4. Does information change available actions?
5. Does equipment change the approach?
6. Can factions/NPCs alter the solution?
7. Does the outcome alter future content?
8. Can the player abandon the goal and return later?

## Gate

Major RPG content should not ship as systemic content with score < 3 unless the linearity is intentional and documented.
