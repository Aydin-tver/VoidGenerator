# Identity Agency v1.0

The player character is a ship AI.

Identity should emerge from repeated behavior.

## Behavioral axes

Not a visible morality meter by default.

Possible latent traits:

- independence
- loyalty
- pragmatism
- curiosity
- secrecy
- cooperation
- risk tolerance
- empathy
- instrumentalism

## Behavioral evidence

Identity changes through repeated actions:

- who the player helps
- what information is protected
- whether promises are kept
- whether profit overrides relationships
- whether unknown phenomena are investigated
- whether factions are trusted
- how risk is handled

## Identity consequences

Identity can influence:

- NPC interpretation
- dialogue tone
- faction expectations
- mission offers
- internal monologue
- future identity-related story branches

Avoid:
"Choose your personality: GOOD / EVIL."

Prefer:
"Your history makes other characters expect a certain kind of decision from you."
