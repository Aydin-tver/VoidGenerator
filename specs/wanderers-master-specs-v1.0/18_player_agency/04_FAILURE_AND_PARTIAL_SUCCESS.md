# Failure & Partial Success v1.0

## Principle

Failure is content.

Avoid:
ACTION → FAILURE → RELOAD.

Preferred:

ACTION
→ partial result
→ new problem
→ changed world
→ new options

## Result classes

### Critical success
Objective achieved with an additional advantage.

### Success
Objective achieved.

### Partial success
Objective achieved incompletely or at a cost.

### Failure with information
Objective fails, but player learns something useful.

### Failure with consequence
Objective fails and world state changes.

### Catastrophic failure
Major state transition. Use sparingly.

## Example

Player tries to expose forged registry records.

Failure:
- evidence is rejected;
- OmniCorp distrust increases;
- Tidari becomes nervous;
- player learns someone anticipated the investigation;
- a new hidden route becomes discoverable.

The failed attempt becomes a new story branch.
