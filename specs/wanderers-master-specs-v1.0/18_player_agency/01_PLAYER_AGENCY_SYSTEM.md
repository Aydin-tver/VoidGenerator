# Wanderers of the Void — Player Agency System v1.0

## Purpose

Stage 18 converts the Stage 17 RPG foundation into a concrete agency model.

The player must not experience the game as:

MISSION → BUTTON → DESTINATION → BUTTON → REWARD.

The target loop is:

OBSERVE → FORM A GOAL → PREPARE → CHOOSE APPROACH → ACT → HANDLE CONSEQUENCES → REASSESS → FORM NEW GOAL.

## Agency pillars

1. Goal agency
2. Approach agency
3. Information agency
4. Capability agency
5. Risk agency
6. Social/faction agency
7. Economic agency
8. Identity agency

## Core rule

A meaningful choice must change at least one future state:

- access
- information
- relationship
- faction standing
- economy
- location state
- mission availability
- equipment opportunity
- future risk
- player identity

A dialogue option that changes only the next sentence is not systemic agency.
