# Approach Agency v1.0

## Required model

A major objective should expose multiple gameplay approaches.

Example objective:
"Determine who altered the convoy route."

Possible approaches:

### Investigation
Collect registry and route evidence.

### Social
Question dispatcher and faction representatives.

### Technical
Use ship scanning/capability equipment.

### Economic
Buy information from traders.

### Faction
Ask a faction intelligence contact.

### Risky direct action
Follow the anomalous route and observe the result.

### Ignore
Do nothing and allow the situation to evolve.

Not every mission needs every approach. Major missions should normally provide 3+ practical approaches.

## Approach quality

An approach is meaningful only if it can change:
- information,
- risk,
- cost,
- relationship,
- timing,
- consequences,
- or future access.

## Capability interaction

Capabilities should modify approaches rather than simply unlock a yes/no door.

Example:

Without advanced scanner:
- inspect manually
- pay a technician
- ask Nova
- accept uncertainty

With advanced scanner:
- perform independent scan
- discover additional evidence
- potentially avoid NPC dependence
