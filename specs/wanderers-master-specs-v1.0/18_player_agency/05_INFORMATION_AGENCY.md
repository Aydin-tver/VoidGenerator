# Information Agency v1.0

Information is a resource and a source of agency.

## Player actions

The player may eventually:

- seek information
- verify information
- compare sources
- withhold information
- disclose information
- trade information
- weaponize information
- intentionally spread misinformation
- protect a source

## Information value

Value depends on:

```text
specificity
+ reliability
+ rarity
+ timing
+ faction relevance
+ economic relevance
```

## Information ownership

Every important knowledge item should record:

- who knows it
- who believes it
- who can verify it
- who benefits from disclosure
- who is harmed by disclosure

## Key design rule

The UI should not always reveal the "correct answer."

It can show:

- source
- confidence
- contradiction
- evidence
- interpretation

The player may then decide what to believe.
