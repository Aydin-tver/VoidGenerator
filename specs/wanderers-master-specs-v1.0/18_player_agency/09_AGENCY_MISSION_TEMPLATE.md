## QA

A major mission should normally satisfy:

- 3+ viable approaches
- 2+ meaningful outcomes
- 1+ non-reward consequence
- 1+ delayed consequence
- 1+ future opportunity or loss
- 1+ approach that uses information
- 1+ approach that uses a gameplay capability
