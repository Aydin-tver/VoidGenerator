# Knowledge-Aware Dialogue v1.0

Dialogue queries the player's knowledge state.

Knowledge may be:

- unknown;
- rumor;
- observed;
- inferred;
- corroborated;
- verified.

NPC knowledge is separate.

Therefore:

PLAYER KNOWLEDGE
≠
NPC KNOWLEDGE
≠
CANON TRUTH

This distinction is essential for mystery gameplay.

## Example

Player knows:
`route_change_signature`

NPC Nova knows:
`ferrum_anomaly`

Neither knows:
`who_created_signature`

Dialogue can allow:
- compare evidence;
- form hypothesis;
- ask indirect questions;
- reveal a contradiction.

The game should not automatically convert evidence into the correct conclusion.
