# Dialogue × Faction Pressure v1.0

NPC conversations are affected by faction context.

An NPC may:
- have an official position;
- privately disagree;
- fear faction leadership;
- protect an internal group;
- possess restricted information;
- be under investigation.

Player may exploit these tensions.

Example:

Tidari officially denies registry manipulation.

Private belief:
the registry was altered.

Player can:
- confront him;
- show evidence;
- offer protection;
- threaten exposure;
- ask indirectly;
- speak to another faction first.

Different approaches produce different information and consequences.
