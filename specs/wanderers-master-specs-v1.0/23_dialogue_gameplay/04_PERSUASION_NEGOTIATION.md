# Persuasion & Negotiation v1.0

Social dialogue uses multiple levers.

## Persuasion inputs

- Social skill;
- evidence;
- reputation;
- relationship;
- faction status;
- demonstrated capability;
- payment;
- leverage;
- shared goals.

## Outcomes

Not binary success/failure.

Use:

1. Refusal
2. Partial cooperation
3. Conditional cooperation
4. Full cooperation
5. Exceptional cooperation

A failed persuasion attempt can still change:
- trust;
- suspicion;
- future price;
- future willingness to talk.

## Negotiation

Negotiation may exchange:
- credits;
- cargo;
- information;
- favors;
- access;
- future obligations.
