# Deception & Information Control v1.0

The player can control information.

Actions:
- lie;
- omit;
- selectively reveal;
- provide misleading evidence;
- tell a technically true but incomplete story.

NPCs evaluate claims using:
- their knowledge;
- trust;
- evidence;
- Social skill;
- faction intelligence;
- previous memories.

## Deception states

Unknown
→ accepted
→ suspected
→ exposed

Exposure creates persistent memory.

A lie that succeeds today can become a liability later.
