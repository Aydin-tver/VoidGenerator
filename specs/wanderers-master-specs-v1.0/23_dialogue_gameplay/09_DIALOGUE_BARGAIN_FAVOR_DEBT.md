# Bargains, Favors & Debts v1.0

Dialogue can create social economy.

Resources:
- favor;
- debt;
- promise;
- protection;
- information;
- access.

Example:

Nova asks:
"Keep this scan out of the registry."

Player can:
- agree;
- demand payment;
- demand future help;
- refuse;
- report it;
- trade the information elsewhere.

A favor creates future state.

Debt should be explicit and traceable, not hidden magic.
