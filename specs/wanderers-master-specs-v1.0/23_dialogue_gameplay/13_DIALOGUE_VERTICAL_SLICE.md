# Stage 23 Dialogue Vertical Slice

## Scenario

Player returns to Nova after discovering evidence connected to the Ferrum anomaly.

### Approach A — Direct question
Requires no special capability.

Result:
Nova gives safe information.

### Approach B — Technical probe
Engineering skill + scan evidence.

Result:
Nova reveals a contradiction.

### Approach C — Persuasion
Social skill + positive relationship.

Result:
Nova shares a confidential scan.

### Approach D — Bargain
Offer protection / future favor.

Result:
Nova gives information but creates a debt.

### Approach E — Deception
Player falsely claims OmniCorp already knows.

Possible:
- cooperation;
- suspicion;
- later exposure.

### Approach F — Threat
Fast information with relationship damage and future consequences.

All approaches converge on the same underlying anomaly but produce different:
- knowledge;
- NPC memory;
- faction exposure;
- future mission conditions.
