# Dialogue × Skills × Equipment v1.0

Skills and equipment can create dialogue options without becoming automatic answers.

## Skills

Social:
- stronger persuasion;
- better reading of motives.

Sensors:
- identify inconsistencies in technical claims.

Engineering:
- challenge technical statements.

Commerce:
- negotiate contracts.

Covert:
- use discreet leverage.

Piloting:
- demonstrate operational credibility.

## Equipment

Evidence scanners, recorders, faction technology or specialized modules can provide leverage.

Rule:

Equipment provides evidence/capability.
Skill affects interpretation/use.
Dialogue choice determines intent.
